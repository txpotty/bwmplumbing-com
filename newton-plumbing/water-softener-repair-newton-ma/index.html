    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Newton

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not.     Resin Bed Problems in Newton

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Newton

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Newton

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Newton, MA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Newton, MA

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Many tenants use a single line. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Newton

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Newton

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Newton

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job.     Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Newton, MA

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Newton

    Many Massachusetts water providers require a yearly test on every testable assembly. Our certified testers connect a calibrated differential gauge to the test cocks and measure the pressure drop across each check valve and the opening point of the relief valve. Each reading has a minimum. RPZs, double checks and PVBs all get tested, and the report goes to your provider.

    Annual Backflow Testing in Newton

    Most water providers require backflow assemblies to be tested at least once a year. We hook up a calibrated differential gauge and measure every check and the relief valve against the required numbers. We fill out the form. A pass means you are set for a year.

    Backflow Preventer Repair and Rebuild in Newton, MA

    Worn check rubbers, grit under a seat and a relief valve that drips are the common failures. We isolate the assembly, take it apart and replace the worn parts with the manufacturer's rebuild kit, cleaning each seat before it goes back together. Then we retest. A rebuild is almost always cheaper than replacement.

    Freeze and Heat Protection for Backflow Preventers in Newton, MA

    Above-ground backflow assemblies sit in the open through summer sun and winter cold. Pipes along exterior walls, in unheated porches and in older homes with little insulation are the most vulnerable. A single hard freeze can crack a brass body or split a bonnet, and years of UV make plastic test cock caps and covers brittle. Covers help a lot. Insulated bags, heated boxes and locking cages are all options.

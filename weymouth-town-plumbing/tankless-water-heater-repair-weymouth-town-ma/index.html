    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Weymouth Town, MA

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Weymouth Town

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Weymouth Town, MA

    Electric units fail in their own way. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Weymouth Town

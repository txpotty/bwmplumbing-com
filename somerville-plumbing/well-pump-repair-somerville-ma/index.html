    Deep Well Pump Repair in Somerville

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Somerville, MA well.

    Emergency Well Pump Repair in Somerville

    No water at all means a household on hold. BWM Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Somerville at the top of the schedule. Technicians carry gauges, an amp clamp and the usual switches and capacitors, so many no-water calls are fixed on the first trip. A full pump swap takes longer.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Somerville

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. BWM Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Somerville, MA well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Somerville, MA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. BWM Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Somerville, MA

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Somerville

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Somerville

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Somerville, MA

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Somerville runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. BWM Plumbing prices both up front. The checkup covers tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap seal, which catches most problems before the pump fails in the middle of a hot week.

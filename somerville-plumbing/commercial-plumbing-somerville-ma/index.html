    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Somerville, MA

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Somerville

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. We also check trap primers.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Somerville

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Our dispatcher will walk your staff to the shutoff over the phone if needed, then send a plumber who can isolate the branch, restore water to the rest of the building and make the permanent repair. Sewage backups get the same response. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Somerville

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Somerville, MA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Somerville, MA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Somerville, MA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow kitchen sinks often mean the trap is full or the baffles are gone. We remind you to keep the pump-out log current for the inspector.

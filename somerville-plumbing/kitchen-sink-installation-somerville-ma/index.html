    Apron-Front Sinks in Somerville, MA

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Somerville, MA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in.     Drop-In Sink Replacement in Somerville

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. The new sink needs to cover the old cutout fully.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Somerville, MA

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Composite Sink Installation in Somerville

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Somerville

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can soak a cabinet in an afternoon. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. For a standard 33 by 22 inch double bowl drop-in, we can often pull the old sink and set a new one the same day.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Somerville

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

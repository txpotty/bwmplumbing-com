    Water Softener Maintenance in Somerville, MA, Part by Part

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Somerville

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Somerville

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Somerville

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Somerville, MA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Somerville, MA

    Plans take the remembering off your plate. You get a reminder call before each yearly visit, and if the control valve fails between visits you get priority booking. Repairs found during a plan visit are billed at a reduced rate.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Somerville, MA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Somerville

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Resin Rebedding in Somerville

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Somerville, MA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Somerville

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. We respond quickly, locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test the utility needs to see before it will restore service. Most are one-visit repairs. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Somerville, MA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Somerville, MA

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Somerville, MA. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Somerville, MA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Snowmelt combined with rain is a common recipe for wet basements, overworked sump pumps and municipal sewer backups.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Somerville

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Somerville

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Somerville, MA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Somerville

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Somerville, MA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Somerville

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Somerville

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Somerville, MA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Somerville

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

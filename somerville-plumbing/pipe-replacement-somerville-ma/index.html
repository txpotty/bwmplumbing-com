    PEX Pipe Installation in Somerville

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Somerville

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. You can usually stay in the house. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Somerville

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Somerville, MA

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Somerville, MA

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Somerville

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, so only part of the building is offline at a time. Night work available.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Somerville

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Somerville, MA

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Somerville

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Grease builds again. Properties with older plumbing or nearby trees do well with periodic cleaning, often jetting, which clears buildup before it turns into a full blockage and extends the life of the line.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Somerville

    Some lines need more than a cable. Jetting handles those. Water at up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, mineral deposits and roots off the pipe wall, cleaning the full diameter instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

    Urgent Sewer Backups in Somerville

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Somerville, MA

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Somerville, MA

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

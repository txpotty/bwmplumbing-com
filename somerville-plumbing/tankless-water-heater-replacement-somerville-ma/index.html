    Picking a Tankless for Your Somerville, MA House

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Somerville

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Somerville

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. That keeps wall patching small. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Somerville

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. That number picks the unit. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Somerville, MA

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. We work after hours when needed.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Somerville

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is the fix, and we can often have a new unit running the same or next day.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Somerville

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Somerville, MA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. We price both.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Somerville, MA

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Expect a full day.

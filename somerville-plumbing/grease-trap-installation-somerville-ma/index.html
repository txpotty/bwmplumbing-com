    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Somerville, MA

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Somerville, MA

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. Plastic tanks in wet soil need hold-down straps or a concrete collar so they do not float when pumped.

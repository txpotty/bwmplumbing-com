    Pipe Repair Options for Lynn, MA Homes

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Lynn

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. More pinholes often follow.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Lynn

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. Pressure comes right back.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Lynn, MA

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a common cause of backups in established neighborhoods. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Lynn

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Buried Line Repair in Lynn

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Pipes along exterior walls, in unheated porches and in older homes with little insulation are the most vulnerable. Service lines fail often. We locate the leak without guessing, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line with a trenchless pull when the old pipe is beyond patching. Drain lines get video first.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Lynn

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Usually a quick fix.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Lynn

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.

    Ceiling and Wall Leak Repair in Lynn, MA

    When water is pouring through a ceiling or spraying under a sink, you need help right away. Locate your shutoff. We walk you through shutting off the water over the phone and send a plumber, and on arrival the plumber repairs the pipe and tests it before turning the water back on. We tell you about wet drywall.

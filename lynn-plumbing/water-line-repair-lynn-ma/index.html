    What Our Water Line Repair Covers in Lynn, MA

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lynn

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Lynn, MA

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. BWM Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Lynn

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and if it keeps creeping we know the leak is between the meter and the house. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Lynn

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Lynn, MA

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. BWM Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Lynn, MA

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Private wells in Massachusetts vary widely, so testing for acidity, iron and other minerals around Lynn is the right start before adding treatment. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Lynn, MA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Lynn

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

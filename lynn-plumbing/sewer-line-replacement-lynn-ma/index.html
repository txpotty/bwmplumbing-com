    Sewer Permits and Code in Lynn, MA

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. Our office files the permit and books the inspection, and our crew meets the inspector so you do not have to. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Lynn

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Lynn, MA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Lynn, MA

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Lynn, MA need lateral work too. Bigger flow and heavier grease mean careful sizing, extra cleanouts for jetting and attention to the grease interceptor tie-in. Some jobs need traffic control. We schedule around tenants and posted hours.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Lynn

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Lynn, MA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

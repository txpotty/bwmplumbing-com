    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Lynn

    Electric water heaters in Lynn usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap.     Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Lynn

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. You shower tonight.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Lynn, MA

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Do not wait it out.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Lynn

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Lynn, MA

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Lynn, MA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

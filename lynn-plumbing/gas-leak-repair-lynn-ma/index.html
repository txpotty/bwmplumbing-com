    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Lynn, MA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Lynn, MA

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company.     Gas Leak Detection in Lynn

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. Slow leaks are what the test gauge is for.     Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Lynn, MA

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. We work from the meter inward, testing each section as we go. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Lynn, MA

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Lynn

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements.

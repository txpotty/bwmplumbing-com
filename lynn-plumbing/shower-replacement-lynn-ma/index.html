    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Lynn

    Shower replacement in Lynn, MA runs from about $1,500 for a basic unit swap to over $10,000 for a custom tiled walk-in. Shower type, materials and plumbing changes drive the price, with slab drain moves adding the most. Tiled showers run higher. You get a detailed quote after we inspect.

    Most replacements wrap up in one to three days. Tile showers and big reroutes often take three to seven days, longer with structural repair. We give you a timeline up front.

    Leaks that come back after repairs are the clearest sign. Cracks in the pan or surround, mold that returns after cleaning, a soft floor near the shower and a drain that clogs constantly all point toward replacement. Some people just want an update.

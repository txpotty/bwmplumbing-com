    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Lynn, MA

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lynn

    Propane homes in Lynn can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Lynn, MA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lynn, MA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Lynn, MA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

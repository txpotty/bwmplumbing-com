    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Lynn, MA

    Finding the frozen spot takes the most skill. Thermal cameras make it faster. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Lynn, MA

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall. You will not see it until the next time you turn on the faucet in spring and water pours into the basement or crawlspace, so we replace split hose bibs with frost-free models that shut off inside the heated wall.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Lynn

    Water heaters in garages or attics have exposed supply lines that can freeze. Tankless heaters on outside walls freeze easily. Freeze protection on tankless units needs power, so after an outage we check the heat exchanger and connectors.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Lynn

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze is cheaper than one burst pipe. Every exposed line gets marked. Attic lines get foam insulation, crawlspace vents get closed or covered, outdoor faucets get swapped for frost-free models, and irrigation lines get drained or blown out before the cold arrives.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Lynn, MA

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Lynn, MA

    Water spraying from a wall or ceiling is a burst pipe. Shut off the main valve now. We replace the split section and test the whole branch, since a freeze often cracks more than one spot.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Lynn, MA

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX off a crimp ring. The split section comes out. Where one split appears, others often follow along the same run, so we pressure test the full branch and replace any section that bulged or thinned instead of patching only the obvious leak.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Lynn

    Foam pipe insulation costs little and prevents most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. Fit matters. We size the sleeve to the pipe, tape every seam and miter the corners so there are no gaps at elbows and tees, since a single bare fitting is often exactly where a line freezes first.

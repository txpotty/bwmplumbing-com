    Mixing Valve Repair in Lynn

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Lynn, MA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Lynn, MA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Lynn, MA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Lynn, MA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Lynn

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Lynn

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Lynn, MA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

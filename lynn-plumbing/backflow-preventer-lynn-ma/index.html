    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Service in Lynn, MA

    RPZ assemblies protect against high-hazard cross-connections at restaurants, medical offices, car washes and buildings with chemical feed. They have two independent check valves with a relief port between them that opens and dumps water if either check starts to fail. Plan a place for the water. We install, test and rebuild RPZ units from Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Febco Backflow Preventer Experts in Lynn

    Febco assemblies are common on irrigation and commercial lines. The 765 PVB, the 850 double check and the 825Y and 860 RPZs are the models we see most. Each has its own quirks.

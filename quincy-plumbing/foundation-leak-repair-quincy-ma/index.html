    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Quincy, MA

    Basement walls in Quincy, MA leak through cracks, tie holes and the joint where the wall meets the floor. Injection fixes cracks.     Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Quincy, MA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Water Line Reroute in Quincy

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Quincy, MA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line.     Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Quincy

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. Most of the time it is a torn lid gasket or a dry floor drain nearby. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Cleaning alone will not fix a bad gasket.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Quincy, MA

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Quincy

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Quincy, MA

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Quincy, MA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Quincy, MA

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Quincy

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

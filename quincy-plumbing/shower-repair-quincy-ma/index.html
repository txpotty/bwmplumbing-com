    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Quincy, MA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Quincy, MA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Quincy

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Most valves we open take a Moen, Delta, Kohler or Pfister cartridge, which means a same-visit fix.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Quincy

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Quincy

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Quincy

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action.     Commercial Shower Service in Quincy

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. After-hours work is available. Each repaired valve gets its limit stop set and tested before we move on.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Quincy, MA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

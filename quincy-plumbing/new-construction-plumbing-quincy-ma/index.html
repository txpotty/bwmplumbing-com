    Plumbing Rough-In Services in Quincy

    Rough-in covers every pipe that ends up inside a wall or floor. We drill joists and studs within the limits the framing code allows, which usually means holes no bigger than 40 percent of a stud's width in a bearing wall. Vents go up through the roof. Each fixture gets a trap arm sized and sloped to its vent distance.

    Water Heater Installation for New Builds in Quincy

    Picking the water heater during design saves a gas line reroute later. A 50-gallon tank covers most three-bedroom homes. Tankless units suit bigger houses but need a larger gas line and their own vent route, both planned at rough-in. We set expansion tanks and pans where code calls for them.

    In-Wall Plumbing Services in Quincy

    Top-out is the second half of rough-in, after framing. We run supply lines, drains and vents through the walls and attic and set every stub-out to the cabinet drawings. Then the system gets tested. We fill the drains to the top fitting and leave a gauge on the supply so the inspector can see it hold.

    Fixture Installation and Trim-Out in Quincy, MA

    After cabinets and countertops are in, we come back for trim-out. Toilets get new wax or foam rings and closet bolts, faucets get braided supplies, and every angle stop gets checked for drips. Every fixture then runs for several minutes. Drywall mud in a trap is common at trim, and we clear it before final.

    Gas Piping for New Homes in Quincy

    We size gas piping for ranges, water heaters, furnaces and fireplaces, plus pool heaters and outdoor kitchens. Each appliance gets a shutoff within six feet of it. Sediment traps go at the furnace and water heater inlets, and the whole system holds a test on a gauge for the inspector before the meter is set. The test runs at least ten minutes.

    Final Trim and Fixture Setting in Quincy

    At trim we install toilets, faucets, garbage disposals and dishwashers, plus ice maker boxes and laundry connections. We let every fixture run long enough to show a slow drip. We also check that hot and cold are on the correct sides, set the water heater to 120 degrees, and clean aerators clogged by construction grit. Then the walkthrough.

    Water Heater and Recirculation Installation in Quincy, MA

    New homes often use a tank heater in the garage or utility room, or a tankless unit on an exterior wall. On tankless units we add isolation valves so the heat exchanger can be flushed without cutting pipe. A recirculation loop is worth considering.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Quincy

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Skip the drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Quincy, MA

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Quincy, MA

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Quincy

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Quincy

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Quincy, MA

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. Mineral scale traps the food. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Quincy, MA

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. You make the call.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Quincy, MA

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    What We Fix on Faucets in Quincy, MA

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Quincy, MA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Quincy, MA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Quincy, MA

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Quincy

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Quincy

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. We find the worn washer, O-ring or cartridge and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Quincy, MA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Quincy

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Quincy

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

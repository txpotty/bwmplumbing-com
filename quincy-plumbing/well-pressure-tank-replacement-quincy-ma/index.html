    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Quincy, MA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Quincy, MA

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Quincy, MA

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Quincy

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Quincy

    Big properties need big drawdown. Stock tanks, irrigation and barns add up. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, sizing them so the pump runs long enough each cycle even under heavy demand. Floor space matters.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Quincy

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Quincy, MA

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. We put in plenty of them. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, which helps the tank resist rust from the inside, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Quincy

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

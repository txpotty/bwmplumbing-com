    Uponor PEX System Installation in Quincy

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Quincy

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. A switch to PEX ends it. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Quincy, MA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. Cold weather slows it. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Quincy

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Quincy

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Quincy, MA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Quincy

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Quincy

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Quincy

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Quincy

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Quincy

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. A new loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 based on access to the main.

    Homes with hard water usually benefit from one. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and a water heater that struggles are common signs. A water test confirms hardness.

    A softener targets calcium and magnesium to stop scale. A filter removes things like chlorine and sediment to improve taste and safety, and plenty of homes use both together.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Plenty of homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring a pro. Bad installs cause leaks, poor softening or slow damage to the plumbing that nobody notices until a fitting lets go behind the water heater. A plumber handles sizing and code.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.

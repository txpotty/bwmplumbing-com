    Toilet Brands We Service in Quincy

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. Put the wrong flapper on one and it will leak within weeks. We match the part to the model number stamped inside the tank and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Quincy, MA

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Quincy

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Sealants and epoxy are a stopgap at best on a tank that holds water every day. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Quincy

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Quincy

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. We clean it with a scrub pad, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. The float gets set so the water level sits roughly one inch under the top of the overflow tube.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Quincy

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Quincy, MA

    An overflowing bowl in a one-bathroom house is an emergency, and so is a burst supply line spraying water across the floor at full house pressure. Shut the angle stop first. Turn the valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise until it is tight, then call us so we can walk you through the rest. The first goal on site is stopping the water, then a permanent repair if the part is on the truck.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Quincy

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

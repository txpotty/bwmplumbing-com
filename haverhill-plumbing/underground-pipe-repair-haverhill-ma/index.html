    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Haverhill

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, so concrete and established plantings stay put. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Underground Water Line Repair in Haverhill, MA

    A leaking water service runs at full street pressure around the clock. Bills climb fast. Low pressure, a wet spot that never dries and a meter that turns with everything off are the usual clues, and we confirm the leak with a pressure drop test before listening along the pipe route to find the exact spot. We splice in new pipe to code.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Haverhill, MA

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. Video locates the break. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, using shielded transition couplings that match the old and new pipe. We confirm slope before backfill.

    Main Water Line Repair in Haverhill

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Leaks never fix themselves. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries tell us to locate, dig and splice the line with code-approved fittings. Rusted steel is better replaced.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Haverhill

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. The homeowner side is yours. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and splice in new PEX or copper with fittings approved for direct burial, then pressure test the repair. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Burst Main Line Repair in Haverhill, MA

    A ruptured main can flood a yard or leave you with no water at all. Locate your shutoff. We help you close the meter valve over the phone, then send a crew to locate and repair the break, while sorting out with the utility whether the leak is on their side. We keep you posted.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Haverhill

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Fixing Showers in Haverhill, MA

    Shower Diverter Repair in Haverhill, MA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Haverhill, MA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Haverhill

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Haverhill

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Haverhill

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Haverhill

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Haverhill

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Haverhill, MA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

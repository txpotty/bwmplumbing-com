    Standard Tank Water Heater Repair in Haverhill

    Most homes still run a 50-gallon tank. Thermostats, elements, anodes and relief valves make up most tank repairs. A good flush takes care of a lot of noise and slow recovery problems on its own, no parts required.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Haverhill

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. The tank gets drained and refilled several times to break up scale, and the drain valve gets cleared. Flushing restores capacity. On hard water, plan on one flush a year.

    Same-Day Emergency Heater Repair in Haverhill

    A tank that is actively leaking or a relief valve that keeps discharging needs prompt attention. We answer emergency calls day and night.     Gas Water Heater Repair in Haverhill, MA

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. The thermocouple and the control valve cause most no-heat calls. After any repair, every gas connection gets a leak test. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Haverhill

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. The replacement parts ride on the truck, which keeps it to one visit.

    Tankless Descaling in Haverhill

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Common causes are scale on the heat exchanger, a dirty inlet filter, a failed flow sensor or a weak igniter. We descale with a pump and approved solution. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Haverhill, MA

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, the drain valve or a supply connection are repairable for a modest cost. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam usually means the inner lining has failed, and that tank needs to be replaced soon. We protect your floors either way.

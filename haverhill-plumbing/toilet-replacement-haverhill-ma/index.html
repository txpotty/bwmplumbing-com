    Common Toilet Replacements in Haverhill, MA

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Haverhill

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Haverhill

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. It gets checked on every replacement. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Haverhill

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Haverhill

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Haverhill, MA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Haverhill, MA

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Current WaterSense models are rated at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Haverhill, MA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Haverhill, MA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

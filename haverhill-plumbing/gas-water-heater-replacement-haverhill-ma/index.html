    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Haverhill, MA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Haverhill, MA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Haverhill, MA

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Haverhill, MA

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. We handle all of it. Downtime stays short.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Haverhill

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. Then it gets installed to code.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Haverhill

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    No Hot Water Calls in Haverhill

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

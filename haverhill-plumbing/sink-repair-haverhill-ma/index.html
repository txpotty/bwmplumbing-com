    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Haverhill

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Haverhill

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year.     Burst Supply Line Repair in Haverhill

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Haverhill

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Local plumbing inspectors enforce the state code consistently, so permitted work is expected to be done to the letter. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Haverhill, MA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Haverhill, MA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Haverhill, MA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Haverhill

    PEX replacements usually run $4,000 to $10,000, and copper often runs $8,000 to $15,000 or more. Attic access matters a lot. Size, fixtures and attic or crawlspace access set most of the cost, and you get a written quote after we see the house.

    Properly installed PEX usually lasts 25 to 50 years. Copper can last longer. Type L copper can last 50 years or more in friendly water, and either one is a large step up from polybutylene.

    Gray or blue plastic pipe stamped PB2110 is the main sign. Cracked fittings are a warning. Since poly can look normal right before it splits, a home from roughly 1978 to 1995 with this pipe should plan a replacement even before any leaks.

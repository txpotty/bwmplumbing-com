    Ejector Pump Jobs We Handle in Haverhill, MA

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Haverhill

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Their packaged systems come with the basin, lid, pump and check valve matched together, which cuts down on field fitting and gives the inspector a clean, listed assembly to look at. A full-port valve gets added.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Haverhill, MA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Haverhill

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Haverhill

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a frequent cause of backups in established neighborhoods. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Haverhill, MA

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. We price both options.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Haverhill, MA

    Businesses in Haverhill, MA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Closed restrooms cost business. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Haverhill, MA

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Haverhill, MA

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

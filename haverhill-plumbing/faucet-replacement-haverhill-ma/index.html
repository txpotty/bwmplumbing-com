    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Haverhill

    Labor for a faucet replacement usually runs $150 to $350. Add a good mid-range faucet and the total typically comes to $300 to $750, depending on the model and whether the stops need replacing. Touchless faucets can pass $800.

    Look under the sink first. Stains on the cabinet floor and crust on the connections usually mean the body leaks, and that is a replacement. Then look at the spout. Cracks in the body or a handle that never fully stops the flow mean it is time.

    Drips that come back after a repair are the clearest sign. Visible corrosion on the body, handles that grind or stick, a base that wobbles on the deck and low flow that cleaning the aerator does not fix all point toward replacement. Some people just want a new style.

    The old faucet leaves with us. Hauling it off is included, and solid brass faucets get recycled as scrap. No cleanup on your end.

    A basic swap is doable with the right tools. Fused nuts and corroded stops are where DIY swaps go wrong, often leaving a cracked sink or a wet cabinet floor. That is a common call for us.

    Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister cover most of what we install. We also put in Grohe and Brizo on remodels, and we install faucets customers buy online as long as the hole layout fits the sink. Fit is what we check.

    About an hour to ninety minutes for most. A seized faucet or new shutoffs can add twenty minutes to forty minutes apiece.

    Count the holes and measure the spacing first. We check your sink's hole count and the spacing between them, then recommend faucets that fit without leaving a gap at the deck. Single-hole models often come with a deck plate to cover extra holes.

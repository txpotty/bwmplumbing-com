    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Springfield, MA: Style by Style

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Springfield

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Springfield

    Drop-in sinks are common in Massachusetts homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Springfield, MA

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Springfield, MA

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.

    Composite Sink Installation in Springfield

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Springfield, MA

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. Many farmhouse sinks drain at the back, and the trap has to work around that.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Springfield

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Springfield get their commercial sinks installed by BWM Plumbing. Hand sinks need hot and cold. Three-compartment, prep and hand sinks go in with the floor sinks, indirect drains and grease trap tie-ins the inspector checks.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Springfield

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

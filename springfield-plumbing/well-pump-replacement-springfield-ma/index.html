    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Springfield

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Springfield, MA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Springfield

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Springfield, MA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Springfield, MA

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Springfield, MA

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. The math does not always work. We will show you the payback.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Springfield, MA

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Springfield, MA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. No more pressure dip mid-shower.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Springfield, MA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Springfield

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Hold off on running water. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Springfield

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Springfield, MA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

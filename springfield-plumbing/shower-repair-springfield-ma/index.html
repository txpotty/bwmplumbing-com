    Fixing Showers in Springfield, MA

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Springfield

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Springfield, MA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Springfield, MA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Springfield

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Commercial Shower Service in Springfield

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. We can work nights. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Springfield, MA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Springfield, MA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Springfield, MA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

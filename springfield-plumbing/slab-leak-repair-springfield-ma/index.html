    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Springfield, MA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Springfield, MA carry parts for both.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Springfield, MA

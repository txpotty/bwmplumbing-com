    Expansion Tank Installation in Springfield, MA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Springfield

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Springfield, MA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Springfield

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Springfield, MA

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Permits are included. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Springfield, MA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Springfield, MA

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Each has its own install manual, and following it to the letter is what keeps the tank warranty valid if something fails in year six. We know those manuals well. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

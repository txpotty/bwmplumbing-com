    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Springfield, MA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Springfield, MA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Massachusetts code before we restart the pump.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Springfield

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Springfield, MA well.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Springfield

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Springfield

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Springfield, MA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Springfield

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Springfield

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Springfield, MA

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Springfield, MA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Springfield

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Springfield, MA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Springfield, MA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Springfield, MA

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. We will say when repair works.

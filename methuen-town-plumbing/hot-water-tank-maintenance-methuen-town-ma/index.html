    Standard Tank Flushing in Methuen Town

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Methuen Town

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. We check static pressure at a hose bib.     Sediment Flushing in Methuen Town, MA

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. That layer insulates water from the heat.

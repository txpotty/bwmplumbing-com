    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Methuen Town, MA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Methuen Town, MA

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and replacement parts are widely stocked. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Methuen Town

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise.

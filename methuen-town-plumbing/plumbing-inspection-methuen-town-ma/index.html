    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Methuen Town, MA

    Humid summers bring condensation on cold water lines and musty basements, which can hide slow leaks until damage appears. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Water Heater Inspections in Methuen Town

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Methuen Town

    Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters.

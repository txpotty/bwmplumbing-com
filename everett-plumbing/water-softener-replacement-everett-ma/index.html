    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Everett, MA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Everett, MA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Everett

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. Scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and insulates the water from the burner, and tankless heaters clog even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Everett

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Everett

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. BWM Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Everett, MA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Everett, MA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Everett, MA

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Everett, MA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. BWM Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

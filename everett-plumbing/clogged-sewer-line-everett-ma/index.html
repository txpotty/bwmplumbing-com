    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Everett

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Everett, MA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Everett

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening. Once inside, they grow into a dense mat that catches toilet paper and waste until the whole line stops flowing. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, then camera the line to see how many joints are letting roots in. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Everett, MA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Everett, MA

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. BWM Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Everett, MA. The camera confirms it is gone.

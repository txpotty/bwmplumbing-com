    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Everett

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Everett

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Everett

    Nor'easters and heavy spring rain can raise groundwater quickly in Massachusetts, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year.     Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Everett, MA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Everett, MA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Everett

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

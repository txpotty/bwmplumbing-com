    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Everett, MA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Everett, MA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a common cause of backups in established neighborhoods. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Everett

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Everett

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning.     Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Everett, MA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Everett

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Everett, MA

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. We rough in valves, supply lines and drains once the walls are framed, set each valve to the depth of the planned finish and locate the drain for the pan you picked. Inspection comes next. We return for trim after tile.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Everett

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

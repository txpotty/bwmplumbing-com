    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Everett

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Epoxy does not hold under water pressure for long. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Everett, MA

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We set the water level to the mark on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Everett

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. The fix is to tighten or rebuild the carrier, then reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Everett

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Everett

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. We clean it with a scrub pad, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. Then we set the float so the water stops about an inch below the overflow.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Everett

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. Should the bowl turn color within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. A new flapper fixes most of these.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Everett

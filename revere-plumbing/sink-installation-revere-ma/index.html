    Pedestal Sink Installation in Revere, MA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing.     Drop-In Sink Installation in Revere, MA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Revere, MA

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. For an accessible bathroom we set the rim no higher than 34 inches, leave knee clearance underneath, and wrap or shield the hot water and drain pipes so a seated user cannot touch them. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Revere

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Revere

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure.

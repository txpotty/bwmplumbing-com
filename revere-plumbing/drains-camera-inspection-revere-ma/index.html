    Identifying Specific Pipe Damage in Revere

    Each kind of damage looks different on camera. Cracks show as dark lines. Offsets show as a lip where one pipe section has dropped below the next, bellies show up as standing water that the camera head dips into, and corrosion on cast iron looks like rough, flaking scale along the bottom of the pipe. The type of damage decides the fix.

    Post-Repair and Post-Jetting Verification in Revere

    Once roots are cut, a line is jetted or a section is repaired, we scope the line again. The before and after speak for themselves. The footage confirms that roots are gone, grease is off the walls and any new section is aligned and sealed, which gives you a record for future reference or a warranty claim. Some leases call for it.

    Kitchen and Branch Drain Camera Inspection in Revere

    Smaller camera heads fit through kitchen, bathroom and laundry lines. They bend through tight turns. We can see grease buildup, scale and broken fittings, plus where each branch ties into the main and whether it slopes the way it should. It is smart before a remodel.

    Identifying Tree Root Intrusion in Revere, MA

    Roots on camera look like fine hairs at first. Then they become a mat. Our footage shows where they come in, how many joints are affected and whether the root mass is a light fringe that cleaning will handle or a dense ball that is already cracking the pipe. That decides the fix. Light roots need cutting, heavy ones need lining or replacement.

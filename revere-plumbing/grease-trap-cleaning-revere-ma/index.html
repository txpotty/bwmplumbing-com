    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Revere, MA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. We take the lid off, remove grease and solids, scrape the baffles, check the flow control fitting on the inlet, and replace the lid gasket if it is flattened or torn. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.

    Commercial Grease Trap Pumping in Revere

    Fryer-heavy menus load a trap fastest. A burger and wings place can fill a 50-gallon trap in two weeks. Our trucks carry enough hose to reach traps tucked behind walk-ins or down in a basement mechanical room, and enough tank capacity for interceptors well over 1,000 gallons. We pump every one to the floor.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Revere

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. On big tanks we break the grease cap with a pressure wand first, since a cap that has set up hard will plug the suction hose and leave half the grease behind. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Revere, MA

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. It adds about an hour.

    Grease Line Jetting in Revere

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. We run a camera after to confirm.

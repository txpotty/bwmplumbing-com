    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Revere

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Revere, MA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Revere, MA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Softener Loop Installation in Revere

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Revere, MA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. Installing on an existing loop is faster and cheaper, while adding a new loop means cutting into the main and running pipe, which adds labor and fittings. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Revere, MA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BWM Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Revere

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Revere

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

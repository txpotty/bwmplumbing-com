    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Revere

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Sectional Point Repair in Revere

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Revere sewer lines.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Revere, MA

    People are often surprised at the final number. Trenchless can cost less. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Revere, MA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Revere, MA soil.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Revere, MA

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Urgent Sewer Failures in Revere

    Sewage backing into a tub is not a wait-and-see problem. Stop running water. We clear the main, scope it the same day and, if a liner or burst is the fix, book it as soon as possible while keeping the line flowing in the meantime so your household can still use the bathroom.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Revere

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

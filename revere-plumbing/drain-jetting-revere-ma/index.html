    Root Cutting With a Jetter in Revere, MA

    Roots get into a sewer through tiny gaps. Mature trees near older clay or cast iron laterals send fine root hairs into the joints, and those hairs grow into a mat that catches paper and solids until the line plugs completely.     Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe.     Jetting Equipment We Use in Revere, MA

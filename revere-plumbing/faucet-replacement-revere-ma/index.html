    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute.     Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Revere, MA

    Emergency Faucet Replacement in Revere, MA

    A cracked faucet body cannot be patched. If water is spraying from the base or the handle and the stop under the sink will not close, shut the main valve where the line enters the house.     Corroded Faucet Replacement in Revere

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Outdoor and Utility Faucet Replacement in Revere

    Hose bibs and laundry faucets get ignored until they leak. Pipes along exterior walls, in unheated porches and in older homes with little insulation are the first to freeze. When a sillcock splits inside the wall, the water can run unseen until a stain shows up on the other side. Frost-free sillcocks go in where pipes can freeze. Laundry tub faucets get a vacuum breaker.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Revere

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. We check that first. Sensor range is set last.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Revere

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. Buckets hung on the spout and hoses yanked off the threads loosen the faucet and eventually crack its supply joints. Brass bodies hold up best here. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

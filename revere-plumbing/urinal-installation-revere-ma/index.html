    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Revere

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Revere

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Revere, MA

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. We install all of them. Spec sheets come first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Revere

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Revere

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Revere, MA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Revere

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Revere, MA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

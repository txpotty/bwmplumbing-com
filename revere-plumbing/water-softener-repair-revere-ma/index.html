    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Revere, MA

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Revere

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Revere, MA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Revere, MA

    Laundromats and restaurants in Revere, MA run through soft water fast, and a dishwasher or boiler starts scaling the moment the softener stops working. Every day of downtime costs money. BWM Plumbing repairs commercial-grade softeners, from twin-tank setups to big valve heads. We schedule around business hours.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Revere

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Revere, MA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Revere

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Revere

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

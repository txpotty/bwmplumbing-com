    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Revere, MA

    Pressure Tank Repair in Revere

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Revere

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Revere, MA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from about $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire size. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. We lay out both options.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Revere

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. BWM Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Revere, MA well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Revere, MA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Massachusetts code when the job is done.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Revere, MA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    No-Water Emergency Repair in Revere

    A dead pump leaves the house without toilets, showers or drinking water. BWM Plumbing answers emergency well pump calls throughout Revere and nearby areas ahead of scheduled jobs. The technician checks power and the switch first. Those two items fix a surprising share of no-water calls without anyone having to touch the pump hanging down in the well.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Revere

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Revere, MA well.

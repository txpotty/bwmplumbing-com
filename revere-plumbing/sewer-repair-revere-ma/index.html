    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Revere, MA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a frequent cause of backups in established neighborhoods. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Revere

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Revere, MA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Revere

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. Older Massachusetts homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

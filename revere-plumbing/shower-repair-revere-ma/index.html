    Mixing Valve Repair in Revere

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. A new cartridge usually fixes it. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Revere, MA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Revere

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Revere

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Revere

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Revere, MA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

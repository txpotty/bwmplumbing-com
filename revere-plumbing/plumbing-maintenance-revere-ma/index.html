    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Revere, MA

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Revere

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Revere

    Sewer problems hide underground. The camera finds roots, grease, sags and offsets before they back up into the house. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a common cause of backups in established neighborhoods. You see the footage. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Revere, MA

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines and old lead service connections are still common in multi-family houses and older single-family homes. Early finds prevent mold. Most leaks we find are small and cheap to fix.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Revere, MA

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Bigger items go on the report.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Revere, MA

    Snowmelt combined with rain is a common recipe for wet basements, overworked sump pumps and municipal sewer backups. Pumps sit unused for months, so we test the float, clean the pit and check that the discharge runs clear. The check valve gets a look. We test any backup pump.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Revere, MA

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across Massachusetts, especially during stretches of single-digit nights and strong wind. We find exposed pipes, unprotected outdoor faucets and lines that need draining before the tough season. Insulation is cheap. Burst pipes are not.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Revere

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. We repair failures. Weather protection gets checked too. Testing on time avoids fines.

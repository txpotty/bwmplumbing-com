    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Revere, MA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Revere, MA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Revere

    Renovations frequently uncover decades of patchwork plumbing, so repiping often makes more sense than repairing one section at a time. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Revere, MA

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Revere

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Revere, MA

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Revere

    Electric water heaters in Revere usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Revere

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Revere, MA

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We lay out the options.

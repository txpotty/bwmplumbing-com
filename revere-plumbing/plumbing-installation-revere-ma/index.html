    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Revere

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight.     Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Revere, MA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Revere

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Sump Pump Installation in Revere, MA

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. The pit goes to proper depth, the primary pump is sized for the inflow, and a battery backup is added on request. Where the water exits matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Revere

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Revere, MA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Revere

    Private wells in Massachusetts vary widely, so testing for acidity, iron and other minerals around Revere is the right start before adding treatment. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. Undersize it and it regenerates all the time, burning through salt. The drain line gets a proper air gap. Then we program the regeneration schedule to your water and run a test cycle.

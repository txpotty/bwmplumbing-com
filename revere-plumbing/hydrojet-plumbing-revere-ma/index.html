    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Revere, MA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Revere, MA

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Revere

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. Floors get protected.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Revere

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Plan on two to three hours.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Revere

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Revere, MA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Root Removal Jetting in Revere

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Revere

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

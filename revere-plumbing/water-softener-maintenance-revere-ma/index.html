    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Revere

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward.     Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Revere, MA

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Revere

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Revere, MA

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Revere, MA

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We will tell you plainly to replace it when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Resin Rebedding in Revere

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

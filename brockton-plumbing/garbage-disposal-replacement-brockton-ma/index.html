    Swapping Out Old Disposals the Right Way in Brockton, MA

    Like-for-Like Disposal Swap in Brockton

    When the old unit and the new one share a mount, the swap is quick and the trap usually lines up without changes. We still replace the flange gasket and putty if they have hardened, and we check the discharge tube for cracks, since old plastic tubes split right at the gasket. Figure on an hour. We haul the old one away.

    Higher Horsepower Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Moving up from 1/3 HP to 3/4 HP cuts down on jams in most kitchens, especially for big families or people who cook from scratch every night. We check the circuit, the cabinet space and the trap height before ordering, because a bigger unit draws more at startup and sits lower in the cabinet. Most kitchens handle it fine.

    Same-Day Disposal Replacement in Brockton, MA

    With water standing in the sink, waiting a week is not an option. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits, so in most cases we diagnose, replace and test on the same visit without a trip to the supply house. Morning calls usually get afternoon slots.

    InSinkErator Model Upgrades in Brockton

    Stepping up from a Badger to an Evolution is the upgrade we install most, since the Evolution line adds grind stages and sound insulation. Both use the Quick Lock mount, so a sound flange and ring can often stay, cutting down the install time. Evolution units are taller. We measure first.

    Mount and Flange Replacement in Brockton

    During every replacement we inspect the sink flange and mount. Corroded, scratched or leaking parts get replaced, which stops top leaks and gives the new disposal a tight seal at the sink. Old putty gets scraped off.

    Waste King and Moen Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Waste King uses the EZ Mount system, which twists on by hand, and many Moen units use a similar style. We match the replacement to the mount when it makes sense, or replace the flange and mount when switching brands, so the new disposal hangs tight with no vibration at the sink. Brand changes take a little longer. The flange has to come out.

    Disposal Replacement With Dishwasher Connection in Brockton, MA

    If your dishwasher drains through the disposal, the new unit's knockout plug has to come out. We reconnect the drain hose with a new clamp and check for an air gap or high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from flowing back into the dishwasher during draining. Both appliances get tested.

    Leaking Disposal Replacement in Brockton

    Water from the bottom of the housing means the internal seal has failed, and that seal is not a replaceable part. We pull the unit, dry the cabinet, check the floor for soft spots and install a new disposal with a new flange and gaskets so every seal on the assembly is fresh. Soft cabinet floors need attention. We point it out before it gets worse.

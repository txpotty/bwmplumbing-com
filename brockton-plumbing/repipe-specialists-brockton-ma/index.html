    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Brockton, MA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Drain Line Repiping in Brockton

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Brockton, MA

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. We cut clean, square openings, protect the area around them and patch every hole once the plumbing passes its test. We match the texture. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Brockton

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Brockton

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    PEX Repiping in Brockton, MA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Copper Repiping in Brockton, MA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

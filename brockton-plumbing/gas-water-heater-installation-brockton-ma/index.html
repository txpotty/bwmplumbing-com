    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Brockton

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Brockton, MA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. BWM Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Then we test draft.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Brockton, MA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Brockton

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Brockton

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Sizing follows household demand.

    Brands We Install in Brockton

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Brockton homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

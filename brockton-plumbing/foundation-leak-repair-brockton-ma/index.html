    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Brockton

    Slab leaks show up quietly. Sometimes all you get is a warm spot on the tile or a bill that creeps up.     Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Brockton, MA

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    Water Line Reroute in Brockton

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Brockton, MA

    Older homes in Brockton, MA often have soft copper running under the slab. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Copper usually pinholes where it rubs on rock. We cut out the damaged length and sweat or press in new Type L copper, then pressure test the whole line before backfilling so you know the repair and the pipe on either side are tight.

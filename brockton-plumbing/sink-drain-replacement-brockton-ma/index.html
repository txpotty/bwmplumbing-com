    Frequently Asked Questions About Sink Drain Replacement in Brockton

    Most sink drain replacements cost $150 to $600. Simple trap swaps cost the least, and full assemblies or wall stub repairs cost the most. You get the price before we start.

    Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Over years, minerals coat the inside of metal drain pipe and shrink the opening. Scale rarely clogs a drain alone, but it catches grease and hair so clogs come faster. Smooth PVC helps.

    Look at the bottom of the trap. Rust spots, white crust around the slip nuts, or a damp ring on the cabinet floor under the J-bend all mean the drain is leaking or close to it, even if you have not seen a drip yet. Press on the trap with your hand. Soft spots mean it is done.

    With a plastic trap, yes. Buy a kit sized to the drain, 1-1/4 inch for bath and 1-1/2 inch for kitchen, line it up so nothing pulls sideways, and hand-tighten the slip nuts plus a quarter turn rather than reaching for pliers. Rusted metal arms are harder.

    Usually the trap has not refilled or a joint is loose. Run water to fill the new trap, then check each slip joint for dampness or a gap. A callback is covered under warranty.

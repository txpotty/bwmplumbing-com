    Pipe Bursting Services in Brockton, MA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Brockton

    Buried sewer damage shows up as odor, slow drains or a soggy patch over the line. Video shows the break. We run a camera with a sonde through the lateral, mark the break on the surface and repair it with a spot dig, a sectional liner or full replacement depending on what the footage shows. Trenchless methods often save Brockton driveways. Not every line does.

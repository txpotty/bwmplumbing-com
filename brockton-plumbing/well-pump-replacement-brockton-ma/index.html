    What We Fix on Well Systems in Brockton, MA

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Brockton

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Brockton, MA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. The suction line gets a pressure test. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Brockton, MA

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. The storage tank does the job a battery would. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Brockton, MA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Brockton, MA

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. You get to see the old part.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Brockton, MA

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. The math does not always work. We will show you the payback.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Lawrence

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Testing comes last. They are dependable and affordable.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lawrence

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lawrence, MA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Lawrence

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Brands We Install in Lawrence

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith, Navien and Rinnai make up most of the gas heaters we install in Lawrence homes and businesses. We compare warranties, recovery and venting across brands so the choice is easy. Budget matters too. We install all of them.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Lawrence

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Some uses need 140 degrees. Installs can happen after close.

    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Lawrence, MA

    Moving up to a bigger or tankless gas heater can outgrow the existing gas line. We size the line from total load and run length so every appliance gets enough gas. Bonding is required on CSST. New lines are pressure tested.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lawrence, MA

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. Endless hot water follows. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. A scale filter may be worth adding.

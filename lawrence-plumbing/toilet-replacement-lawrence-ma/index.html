    Types of Toilet Replacements in Lawrence, MA

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Lawrence

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Lawrence

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Lawrence, MA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Lawrence

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. The cracked unit comes out, and we look at the floor under it before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Lawrence, MA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. We can replace several toilets in one visit. Tenants keep a working bathroom. Entry gets coordinated with each tenant directly, so you do not have to be there.

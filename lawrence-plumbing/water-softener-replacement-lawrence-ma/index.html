    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Lawrence

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Lawrence, MA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Lawrence, MA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Lawrence

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Lawrence

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Lawrence

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Lawrence

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Lawrence, MA

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. That is how we compare models.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Lawrence

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Lawrence

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Lawrence

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Lawrence

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Lawrence, MA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. Then we find out why it failed.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Lawrence

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Takes about two hours.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Lawrence

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

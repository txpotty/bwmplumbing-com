    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Lawrence, MA

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Lawrence

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Lawrence, MA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

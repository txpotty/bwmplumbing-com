    Tankless Descaling and Service Work in Lawrence, MA

    Home Tankless Flushing in Lawrence

    Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Lawrence

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Lawrence, MA

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. A descaling visit costs a fraction of that. With yearly care, a tankless unit can last close to 20 years. We can also tell you whether a softener or scale inhibitor would pay off in your house.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Lawrence

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Lawrence, MA

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Lawrence

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Lawrence, MA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Lawrence

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

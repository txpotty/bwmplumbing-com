    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Lawrence

    Slab leaks show up quietly. Sometimes all you get is a warm spot on the tile or a bill that creeps up. BWM Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to locate the break under your concrete.     Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Lawrence, MA

    Older homes in Lawrence, MA often have soft copper running under the slab. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Copper usually pinholes where it rubs on rock. We cut out the damaged length and sweat or press in new Type L copper, then pressure test the whole line before backfilling so you know the repair and the pipe on either side are tight.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Lawrence

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. Cutting one is dangerous. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Lawrence, MA

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut.     PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Lawrence

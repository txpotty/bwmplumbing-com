    Where Drain Jetting Helps in Lawrence, MA

    Root Cutting With a Jetter in Lawrence, MA

    Roots get into a sewer through tiny gaps. Mature trees near older clay or cast iron laterals send fine root hairs into the joints, and those hairs grow into a mat that catches paper and solids until the line plugs completely. A spinning cutter shreds the mat. A camera pass shows which joint they came through and whether a repair will stop them for good.

    Commercial Kitchen Drain Jetting in Lawrence, MA

    Prep sinks, dish lines and floor sinks all carry grease. Even with a properly sized interceptor, the branch lines inside the building build up fast, and a busy kitchen can go from fine to flooded floor sinks in a single weekend. We put it on a schedule. Work happens before opening or after close. You get a record for inspections.

    Jetting Hard Water Scale in Lawrence

    Galvanized and cast iron drains collect mineral scale. That rough surface grabs hair, grease and soap every time water passes, so the clogs come back faster each year even if nobody changes their habits. We use a scale-cutting head. The camera tells us how much wall remains under the scale.

    Restaurant Grease Line Jetting in Lawrence

    Floor sinks backing up during service shut a kitchen down. Cooled grease narrows the line to the interceptor until each pot wash floods the floor. We jet with hot water. Inspectors notice clean floor sinks.

    Grease Line Jetting in Lawrence, MA

    Grease turns hard once it cools. The line between the sinks and the interceptor gets packed with cooled grease until floor sinks overflow. We often jet with hot water. Regular jetting keeps the kitchen open and the interceptor working the way it should.

    Jetting Versus Snaking Costs in Lawrence, MA

    Jetting costs more per visit than a cable. Three or four cable calls a year usually cost more than one jetting visit that lasts. For a single hair clog, a cable is fine. We quote both on site.

    Bathroom Branch Jetting in Lawrence

    Hair and soap scum coat bathroom lines. When the sink, tub and shower on one branch all drain slowly at once, the shared pipe behind the wall is usually coated, and a hand auger through one fixture will not reach it. A jetter can. Every fixture gets tested before we pack up.

    Jetting Equipment We Use in Lawrence, MA

    Different lines need different machines. Our compact jetters run around 1,500 to 3,000 PSI on small hose for branch drains, while our sewer units push up to 4,000 PSI with much higher flow for 4 and 6 inch lines. Nozzles make the difference. Penetrating heads bore through packed clogs, rear-jet heads scrub walls, and spinning cutters handle roots.

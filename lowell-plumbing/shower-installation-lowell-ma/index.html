    Walk-In Shower Installation in Lowell

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Lowell

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Lowell, MA

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. We work around your business. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Lowell, MA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Lowell

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. The custom shower comes later. The framing gets inspected first.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Lowell

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Lowell, MA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Shower Pan Installation in Lowell

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

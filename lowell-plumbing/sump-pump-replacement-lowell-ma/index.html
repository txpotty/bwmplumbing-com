    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Lowell, MA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs.     Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Lowell

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Lowell, MA

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Lowell

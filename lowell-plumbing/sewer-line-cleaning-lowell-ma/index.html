    Clearing Household Laterals in Lowell

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots come first. Flushed wipes, built-up kitchen grease and root intrusion account for most backups, and we locate the problem on camera before choosing between snaking and jetting, so the fix lasts.

    Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Lowell, MA

    Sewer backups pick bad times. Holidays are common. Our emergency team handles urgent blockages and overflows with main line equipment and cleanup gear, getting the line open and limiting damage as quickly as possible.

    Commercial Sewer Line Cleaning in Lowell, MA

    Commercial laterals see heavy use. Restaurants fight grease daily. Higher volumes of waste and commercial-grade blockages call for heavy-duty equipment, and we schedule the work to keep downtime short so the business stays open.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Lowell, MA

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Sewer odors, slow drains in several rooms and water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes are all warning signs.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Lowell, MA

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. They enter at clay joints and small cracks, so we cut them out with root saws and finish with jetting to remove the fine hairs left behind, then show each entry point on camera.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Lowell

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Lowell

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Lowell, MA

    A waterproof camera shows the pipe condition. Cracks and breaks show up. The video shows what is wrong and where, so we can recommend the most cost-effective fix.

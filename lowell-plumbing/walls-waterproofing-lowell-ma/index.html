    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Lowell, MA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries.     Basement Wall Sealing in Lowell, MA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Water heaters and boilers run long hours through the winter, which shortens their life compared to milder parts of the country. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Lowell

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Lowell, MA

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, a white powder on block or brick called efflorescence, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Stair-step cracks and bowing walls also deserve a closer look.

    Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on weather and how deep the footing sits. We give you a timeline at the first visit.

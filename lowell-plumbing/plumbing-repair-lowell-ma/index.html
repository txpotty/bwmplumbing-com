    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank.     Water Leak Repair in Lowell

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down.     Well Pump Service in Lowell, MA

    Submersible pumps sit well below the water line, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. BWM Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Lowell, MA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Both skip the trench through your driveway and landscaping. Once restoration is counted, the price is often close to a deep dig. A camera decides.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Lowell

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back.

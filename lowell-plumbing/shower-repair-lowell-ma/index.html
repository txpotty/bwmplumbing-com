    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Lowell

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head.     Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Lowell

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Lowell

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize.     Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Lowell

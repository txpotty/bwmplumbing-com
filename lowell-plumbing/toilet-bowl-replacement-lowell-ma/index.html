    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Lowell, MA

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Lowell

    Floor-mount commercial bowls take a beating in high-traffic restrooms, and the ones that crack usually do it at the bolt holes from years of being kicked or overtightened. We match the outlet and spud size. The flushometer gets reconnected and tested before the restroom reopens.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Lowell, MA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Lowell, MA

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. Bowl and tank have to match. Upgrading an old toilet usually means replacing both, and we will price it that way.

    Brands We Match in Lowell

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. Close is not good enough. The pairing gets confirmed from the model number before we order, which avoids a second trip.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Lowell

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Some bowls are not sold on their own. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Lowell

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not. Some seep for weeks and some let go in a second. We drain the tank, lift the tank off and remove the bowl, and the matching bowl goes in on a fresh seal with new tank gaskets and bolts.

    Changing Toilet Bowl Styles in Lowell

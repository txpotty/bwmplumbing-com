    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Lowell

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Lowell

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Lowell

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Lowell, MA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Waltham, MA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Waltham, MA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Waltham

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Waltham, MA

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue.

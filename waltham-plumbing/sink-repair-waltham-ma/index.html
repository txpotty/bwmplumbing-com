    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Waltham, MA

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Waltham

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute.     Hair Clog Clearing in Waltham

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Waltham

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Waltham, MA

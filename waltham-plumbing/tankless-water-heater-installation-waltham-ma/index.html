    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Waltham, MA

    Upfront, a tankless install in Waltham, MA costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.     Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Waltham, MA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Waltham, MA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Waltham, MA

Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Waltham

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

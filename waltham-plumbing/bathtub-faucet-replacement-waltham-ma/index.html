    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Waltham

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Waltham

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Waltham

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Waltham, MA

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. Plaster cracks wider than drywall when you cut it, so we open plaster carefully with a multi-tool and often go in from an adjacent closet, and the heavy tub stays put while we work on the valve above it. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Waltham

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Waltham, MA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Waltham, MA

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. A plumber heads out as soon as one is free. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Waltham

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Waltham, MA: The Details

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Waltham

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Waltham, MA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Waltham, MA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Waltham, MA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Waltham

    Drop-in sinks are common in Massachusetts homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Waltham

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Waltham, MA

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Waltham

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

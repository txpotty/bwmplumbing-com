    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Waltham, MA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Waltham

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Waltham

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier.

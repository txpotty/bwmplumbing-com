    Walk-In Shower Installation in Waltham

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Waltham, MA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Waltham

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on.     Custom Shower System Installation in Waltham, MA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch.     Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Waltham

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning.     Steam Shower Plumbing in Waltham

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

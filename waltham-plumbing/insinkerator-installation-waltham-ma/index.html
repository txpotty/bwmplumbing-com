    InSinkErator Installation Done Right in Waltham, MA

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Waltham

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Waltham, MA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Waltham

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. That seal cannot be fixed. We disconnect the old unit, cap the dishwasher hose while we work and set the new disposal on the same drain geometry, adjusting the tailpiece if the new one sits at a different height.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Waltham

    Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Waltham, MA

    Price depends mostly on two things: the unit you pick and how much needs to change under the sink. A like-for-like swap is the low end. A first-time install that needs a new flange and a new outlet costs more, because that is real labor and sometimes an electrician's visit too. You get the number before we start.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Waltham

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Waltham, MA

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. With the motor running, we check the mount for vibration.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Waltham, MA

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. The cheapest job is a Badger 5 swap with a cord kit. An Evolution Excel in a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lowered trap arm sits at the top, and most jobs land somewhere between. You see horsepower and warranty length side by side.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Malden

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Malden

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition.     Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Malden

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Malden

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Malden

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, then dig a small pit at the mark or pull new pipe through the old path when the line is too far gone to patch. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Buried Line Repair in Malden

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across Massachusetts, especially during stretches of single-digit nights and strong wind. Water mains are common. We locate the leak without guessing, and either fix it at the mark or pull a new line through the old route. Drain lines get video first.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Malden

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Malden, MA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from about $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire size. An older pump that already needs pulling is often worth replacing on the same trip. We lay out both options.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Malden

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Malden, MA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Malden, MA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. BWM Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Malden, MA

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and BWM Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Parts for the common models ride on the truck. A pump with life left in it is worth repairing, and that saves the cost of a new motor and the labor to set it.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Malden

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Malden, MA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Malden, MA

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Malden, MA

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Malden, MA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Malden

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Malden, MA

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Pipes along exterior walls, in unheated porches and in older homes with little insulation are the most vulnerable.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Malden, MA

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. You get a straight answer on whether your pump has life left or should be replaced.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Malden, MA

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. Often it is just a tripped GFCI or a hung float. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Malden

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Malden

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Malden, MA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Massachusetts homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Malden

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Malden, MA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Massachusetts homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Malden

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Use pellet salt.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Malden

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

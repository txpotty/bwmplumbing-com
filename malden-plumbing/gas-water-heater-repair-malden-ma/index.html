    Propane Water Heater Repair in Malden

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. A propane tank needs its own orifice and a valve set for roughly 11 inches of water column. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.

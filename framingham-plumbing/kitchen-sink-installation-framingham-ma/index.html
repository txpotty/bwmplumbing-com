    Apron-Front Sinks in Framingham, MA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured.     Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Framingham

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first.     Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Framingham

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently.

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Framingham, MA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Framingham

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

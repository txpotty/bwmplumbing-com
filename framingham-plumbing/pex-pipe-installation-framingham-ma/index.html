    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Framingham, MA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Framingham

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Framingham, MA

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. We pull the burner assembly and brush it clean. We also check the thermocouple or flame sensor, since a weak one lets the pilot go out every few days and leaves you resetting it on your knees. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Framingham

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. Those chunks cause the popping. For stubborn scale we use a potable-rated descaler, then flush it clean. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Framingham

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Framingham, MA

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. You get a reminder call when the flush is due. Plans cover one or two visits a year at a fixed rate, with priority scheduling if the heater fails between visits and a discount on parts like anodes and drain valves. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Framingham

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. Some models hide the anode under the top cover. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. Our trucks carry sockets for each brand.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Framingham, MA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We replace it with an aluminum-zinc rod or a powered anode, then chlorinate the tank to kill the bacteria living in it. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Emergency Tank Inspections in Framingham

    Sudden changes call for a look the same day: a tank that suddenly rumbles, hot water that turns brown, a puddle forming under the pan, or a relief valve that will not stop dripping. We diagnose first. The fix may be a flush and a drain valve, or the liner may be gone.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Framingham

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Framingham, MA

    Humming Disposal Repair in Framingham

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Framingham, MA

    If water is reaching the floor, that comes first. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. After that, call. Most times we stop the leak that day and either fix the disposal or price a new one on the spot.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Framingham, MA

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Framingham

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Framingham

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Framingham, MA

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Framingham, MA

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Framingham

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Massachusetts plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

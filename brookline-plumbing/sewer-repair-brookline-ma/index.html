    Sewer Repair Methods for Brookline, MA Homes

    Root Intrusion Removal in Brookline, MA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Brookline yards make this a common call.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Brookline

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Brookline, MA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Brookline

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Brookline, MA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Nearly every older home has a basement, so foundation drainage and sump pumps matter for keeping the lower level dry. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Brookline, MA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Brookline, MA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Brookline we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and coordinate with property managers on tenant notices. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Brookline

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. Daylight digs are safer around buried utilities.

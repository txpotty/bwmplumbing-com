    Water Softener Replacement Services in Brookline, MA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Brookline

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff.     Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Brookline, MA

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Brookline, MA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BWM Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Replacement in Brookline, MA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Brookline

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Brookline

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Brookline, MA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Brookline, MA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Brookline

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Brookline

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Brookline, MA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Brookline, MA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Brookline

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Burst Main Line Repair in Brookline, MA

    A ruptured main can flood a yard or leave you with no water at all. Find the meter valve. We help you close the meter valve over the phone, then send a crew to locate and repair the break, while sorting out with the utility whether the leak is on their side. We keep you posted.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Brookline

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. You own that part. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Brookline

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Brookline, MA

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Old pipe gives up. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets Massachusetts' plumbing code.

    Emergency Underground Pipe Repair in Brookline

    A burst service line can flood a yard and wash out the soil under a driveway. Turn off water at the meter. We talk you through the shutoff on the phone and send a crew to locate and expose the break, then repair it and restore water as fast as the dig and the fittings allow. Most get fixed the same day.

    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in Brookline

    HOA common areas and apartment complexes have long buried runs under parking and sidewalks. Tenants still need to get in. We locate carefully to avoid unnecessary concrete cutting, schedule work around residents, and document every repair with photos and test results that property managers can hand to the board or the insurer. Night work is an option.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Brookline, MA

    Pool auto-fills, guest houses, outdoor kitchens and detached garages often have their own buried water lines. They are hard to trace. We use tracer wire, electronic locators and acoustic gear to find leaks along the full length, using buried-rated pipe and a pressure test before backfill. Rerouting can be cheaper.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Brookline, MA

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. When they fail, water pools. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and repair broken runs so runoff gets carried off instead of sitting next to the house. The outlet gets cleared too.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Brookline

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Big jobs climb higher. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. The price comes after we look.

    Our labor warranty is written into the quote. Length depends on the job. A spot repair and a full service replacement carry different coverage since each job uses different methods and parts. Manufacturer warranties stack on top.

    It depends on your policy and the cause. Read the exclusions. Sudden and accidental damage is sometimes covered, but normal aging and long-term leaks generally fall outside coverage. Call your agent to confirm. We provide documentation for the claim.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. We also use tracer gas. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, while a locator shows the pipe path for the survey. Together they narrow it to a few feet.

    Yes, every common material. Old and new. PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX, clay, galvanized and polybutylene all come up, which means matching couplings and methods to whatever is already in the ground. Common fittings ride on the truck.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, and every one of these holds up to soil and water far better than galvanized or clay. We follow your city's code.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Service lines are different. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marks and code-compliant fittings rated for burial, so those should go to a licensed plumber. Any dig needs 811 first.

    A minor leak repair can take a few hours. Bigger jobs run a day or more. A full trenchless sewer replacement could take one to three days, depending on the length of the line and how long the inspection takes to schedule. The timeline is in the estimate.

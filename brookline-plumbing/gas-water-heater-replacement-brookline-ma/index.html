    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Brookline, MA

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Brookline

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Brookline, MA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. Choosing is simple.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Brookline

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Brookline

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Brookline, MA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Brookline

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Massachusetts plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Brookline

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

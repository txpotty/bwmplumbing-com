    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Brookline, MA

    Grinding Noise Repair in Brookline

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Brookline

    A dead disposal during dinner prep is a real problem, especially if the sink is full of water. Skip the drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Brookline, MA

    Most jams and small leaks are much cheaper than a replacement. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. You make the call.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Brookline

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Brookline, MA

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Brookline

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Brookline, MA

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Brookline

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.

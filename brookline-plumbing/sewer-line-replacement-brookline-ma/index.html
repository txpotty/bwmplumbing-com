    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Brookline, MA

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Brookline

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Sewer Pipe Materials We Install in Brookline, MA

    PVC covers most of our trenched work. SDR 35 is the common sewer grade for buried laterals, Schedule 40 shows up where code or the inspector wants a heavier wall, and HDPE is our pick for bursting. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a frequent cause of backups in established neighborhoods. Cast iron still gets used indoors on some jobs. The material choice depends on depth, soil and the method you pick.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Brookline

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. The tech scopes the line, marks the break and lets you know whether the dig can start next day or has to wait on locates. Utility marking takes a few days in most areas. We can often set up a bypass so one bathroom stays usable.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Brookline

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Rough scale catches everything. We swap the outdoor section for plastic pipe. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Brookline, MA

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings. Far cheaper than a full run. We only suggest it when the footage backs it up.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Brookline, MA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Brookline

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. A camera that dead-ends at broken clay or squashed fiber pipe means we have to dig, clean out the debris and replace that run. Digging also lets us see the soil around the break. Sometimes we find a washout or a root mat that explains why the pipe failed in the first place.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Brookline, MA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Brookline, MA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Brookline, MA are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Brookline, MA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Brookline, MA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Brookline

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Brookline

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Brookline

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Brookline, MA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. You buy more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Brookline, MA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

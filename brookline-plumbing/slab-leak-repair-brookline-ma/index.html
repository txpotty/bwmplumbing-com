    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Brookline, MA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. We dig in from outside. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Brookline, MA homes are good candidates.

    Slab Leak Detection in Brookline

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Brookline

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Brookline home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Brookline

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Brookline homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Brookline, MA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Brookline homes.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Brookline, MA

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

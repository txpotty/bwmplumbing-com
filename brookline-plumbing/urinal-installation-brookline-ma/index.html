    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Brookline, MA

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Brookline, MA

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Brookline, MA

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Brookline, MA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Brookline

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Brookline

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Brookline

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. The carrier gets set for a 17 inch rim, the floor space stays clear, and the flush control stays reachable. Measurements get checked twice. Inspectors measure too.

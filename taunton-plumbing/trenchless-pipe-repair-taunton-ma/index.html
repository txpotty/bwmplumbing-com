    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Taunton

    Older Taunton homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Taunton

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

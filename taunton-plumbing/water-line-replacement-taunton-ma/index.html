    PEX Water Line Installation in Taunton, MA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across Massachusetts, especially during stretches of single-digit nights and strong wind. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Taunton, MA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Taunton, MA

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Taunton

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Taunton, MA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

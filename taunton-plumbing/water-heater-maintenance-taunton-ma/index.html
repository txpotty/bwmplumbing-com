    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Taunton

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion.     Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Taunton

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

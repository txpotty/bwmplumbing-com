    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Taunton, MA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Taunton, MA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Taunton

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall.     Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Taunton

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall.     Keeping a New Sewer Line Healthy in Taunton

    New pipe still needs a little care. Plant thirsty trees away from the line, since big roots can push on even a sound pipe. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a frequent cause of backups in established neighborhoods. Flushable wipes still clog.     Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Taunton

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. PVC and HDPE do none of that. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Taunton, MA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Taunton, MA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Taunton, MA

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Taunton

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Taunton

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Taunton, MA

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Taunton, MA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Taunton, MA

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.

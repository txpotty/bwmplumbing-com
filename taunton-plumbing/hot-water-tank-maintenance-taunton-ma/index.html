    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Taunton, MA

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not.     Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Taunton

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Taunton

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. We check static pressure at a hose bib. Then we lift the relief valve lever. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.

    Relief Valve Testing in Taunton

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

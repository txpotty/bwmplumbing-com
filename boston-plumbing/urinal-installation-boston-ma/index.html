    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Boston, MA

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Boston, MA

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Boston

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Boston

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We lay out each urinal at 30 inches center to center and 15 inches from the side wall, set the carriers, and run waste, vent and supply so the finish crew can hang fixtures as soon as tile is done. Coordination with the GC is constant. Rough inspection comes first.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Boston, MA

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Boston, MA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Boston

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Boston

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. A single urinal at a busy bar can flush a few hundred times on a Friday night, so we install fixtures and valves that recover quickly and stand up to rough handling without loosening on the wall. Vandal-resistant valves are available. Night installs keep you open.

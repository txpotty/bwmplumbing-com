    What Our Sewer Clearing Covers in Boston, MA

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Boston

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Many times they team up. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Boston

    Older Boston homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. Old joints let them in. BWM Plumbing cuts roots out with a sectional machine and a root saw sized to the pipe, then jets the line to wash out the cut roots. Root treatment can slow regrowth between visits.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Boston

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, and the transmitter lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above it. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Boston, MA

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Then it grows. Soap scum and food bind to it until the opening shrinks to a fraction of the pipe, after which ordinary use causes backups. BWM Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Boston, MA. Then we show you on camera.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Boston, MA

    Nearly every older home has a basement, so foundation drainage and sump pumps matter for keeping the lower level dry. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, so the clog keeps returning in the same place. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.

    Emergency Clogged Sewer Line Repair in Boston, MA

    Sewage backing up into the house is an emergency. Quit flushing and running water. BWM Plumbing answers after hours in Boston, MA and sends a tech with a sewer machine, a jetter and the gear to contain the mess and disinfect the area once the line is open.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Boston, MA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Boston

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. Each clog costs more without one. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, and a two-way cleanout by the foundation fixes that. It pays for itself on the next call.

    A Sewer Technician Near You in Boston, MA

    Nearby also means we know how Boston sets up its sewer taps. It speeds things up. When the camera shows the clog is past your property line, we can tell you right away and help you report it to the city instead of paying for work on their pipe.

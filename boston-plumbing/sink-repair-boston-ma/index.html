    What We Fix Under the Sink in Boston, MA

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Boston, MA

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Boston, MA

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Boston

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Boston

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Boston, MA

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Boston

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Boston

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Boston

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Guessing wastes drywall. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, and we isolate sections of the plumbing with pressure tests so the search narrows to one wall or one floor before anything gets opened. One cut, one fix.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Boston

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Buried Line Repair in Boston

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Shutting off and draining outdoor faucets in the fall, and keeping cabinet doors open during cold snaps, prevents a lot of winter damage. Water mains are common. We locate the leak without guessing, and either fix it at the mark or pull a new line through the old route. Sewer lines get a camera.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Boston, MA

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Nearly every older home has a basement, so foundation drainage and sump systems matter for keeping the lower level dry. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Boston

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. You decide the timing.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Boston, MA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BWM Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

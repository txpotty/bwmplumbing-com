    Pool Leak Detection in Boston

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Boston

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Boston, MA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Boston, MA

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Boston pay for every hour a leak keeps running. Sprinkler lines, cooling tower makeup water and long copper mains are common sources, and a leak in any of them can run under a floor or above a drop ceiling for weeks. Each zone gets isolated and tested, then mark leaks for repair. Most tenants never see us.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Boston

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Boston, MA

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. The bill usually tells you first. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Boston home.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Boston, MA

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, with a locator giving the exact depth and distance.

    Water coming through a ceiling at night does not wait for an appointment. Shut the main. We respond to Boston, MA emergencies with full detection gear, find which line let go, and isolate it so you can get water back on the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps without a change in use, damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, or water running with every fixture off. A moving meter is the clearest sign. Shut everything off and watch it for ten minutes.

    Shut every fixture off and watch the meter. If the dial or the little triangle leak indicator moves at all over ten minutes, water is escaping somewhere in the system. So is a sudden jump in your bill.

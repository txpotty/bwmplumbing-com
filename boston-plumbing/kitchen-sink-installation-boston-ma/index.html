    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Boston

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Boston

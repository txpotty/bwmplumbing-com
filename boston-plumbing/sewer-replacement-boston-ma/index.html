    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Boston

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and coordinate traffic control if the tap sits in the street, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Boston, MA

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, and a clean pipe gives a much more honest picture of its condition. Guesswork costs money.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Boston

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Boston

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Boston

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. It records every joint, crack and root on the way to the main. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, so we can find its exact spot and depth from the yard above, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Boston, MA

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Boston

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Boston, MA

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Sewer Replacement Near Me in Boston, MA

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. On sewer jobs that is a big deal. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in Boston around the inspector's visit and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.

    Our crews work on sewer lines across Boston every week. Neighbors usually have the same pipe material, since streets were built together. If the house next door had clay pipe full of roots, yours probably does too, which gives us a good guess before we scope. Patterns help.

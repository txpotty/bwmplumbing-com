    After-Hours Tankless Repair in Boston, MA

    Some tankless failures will not wait for Monday. A leaking heat exchanger or a gas smell near the unit means we come out after hours in Boston. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call the gas utility from outside, then call us, and our tech can close the gas cock at the unit and shut the water valves until the part arrives.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Boston, MA

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Boston

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Boston

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Boston, MA

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Boston

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. A code 10 can also come from a vent run that is too long for the model. We measure.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Boston, MA

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and we test every one against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Boston

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

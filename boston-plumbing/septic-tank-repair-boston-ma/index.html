    Drain Field Line Jetting in Boston

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Boston

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Boston, MA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Boston, MA

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Boston, MA

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Grease from a kitchen and paper towels from restrooms are the usual troublemakers. Our fix often includes a bigger effluent filter and a pumping schedule tied to real use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Boston, MA

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Boston

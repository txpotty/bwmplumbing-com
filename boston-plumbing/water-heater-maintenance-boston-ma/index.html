    What a Water Heater Service Visit Covers in Boston, MA

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Boston

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. The tank heats better. So does recovery time.

    Anode Rod Checks in Boston

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Boston, MA

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. We service them regularly. We keep your business supplied.

    Tankless Descaling in Boston, MA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Boston, MA

    Small drips become floods. Connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the seams get checked, along with the pan and floor. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Boston

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Boston, MA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Boston, MA

    Something sounds or feels wrong. If the tank is rumbling, the hot water ran out early or there is water in the pan, we can come out, find the cause and tell you whether it is a simple repair or a sign the tank is near the end. You get options. No pressure either way.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Boston, MA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Boston, MA

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. We clear it first. Usually a cable gets the water moving, and then we jet the line to remove what caused the backup, whether that is a grease plug, a root mass or a buildup of wipes caught on a rough joint.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Boston, MA

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Boston

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Boston

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Boston

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Boston, MA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. You can see the change on video.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Boston, MA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Hydrojet Service Near Me in Boston, MA

    Hiring BWM Plumbing locally means we can return and compare camera footage over time. The comparison tells a story. Seeing a root mass at the same joint two years in a row, a little bigger each time, makes it easy to decide when a spot repair is worth more than another round of jetting.

    A local jetting crew has a good idea of the pipe before arriving. Older areas mean clay and cast iron. Newer neighborhoods have PVC that takes full pressure, while older ones need gentler settings, and knowing that before we arrive means the right nozzles are already on the truck.

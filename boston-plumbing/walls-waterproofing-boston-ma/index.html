    Basement Wall Sealing in Boston, MA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Long, cold heating seasons are the main stress on plumbing in Massachusetts, with months of near-freezing water entering the home. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Drainage System Integration in Boston, MA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Boston, MA

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Boston, MA

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water.

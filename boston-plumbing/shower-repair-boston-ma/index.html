    Shower Valve Replacement in Boston

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve.     Dripping Showerhead Repair in Boston

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Boston, MA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Boston, MA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Boston, MA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Boston, MA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner.     Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Boston, MA

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Boston

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Boston, MA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in Boston

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day.     Water Softener and Filtration Service in Boston

    Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. We program regeneration to your household's use.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Boston, MA

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs.     Faucet and Fixture Repair in Boston

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet.

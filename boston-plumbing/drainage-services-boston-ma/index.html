    Dry Well Installation in Boston, MA

    Where there is no place to discharge on the surface, a dry well lets water soak into deeper soil. Drainage depends on the soil. We run a percolation check before digging, size the well for the roof or yard area feeding it, and keep it at least ten feet from the foundation and well away from any septic field.

    Fixture Drain Clearing in Boston

    Kitchen sinks clogged with grease and bathroom drains slowed by hair make up most of our fixture calls. Pulling the trap is often the fastest fix. Toilets get a closet auger, and if that fails, we pull the toilet and clear it from the flange.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Boston, MA

    Recurring drain trouble needs a camera to see what is going on. We push a waterproof camera through the cleanout and record the trip to the street, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth of any problem on your lawn. You get the video. Buyers often order one before closing.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Boston, MA

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A channel drain catches that sheet flow. We saw-cut the slab, set a polymer or concrete channel with a grate rated for the traffic it will see, and pipe it in solid PVC to a safe discharge point away from the foundation.

    French Drain Installation in Boston

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Boston

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in Massachusetts, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and more expensive. Underground downspout lines carry roof water to a pop-up emitter or dry well, with a cleanout for flushing. One inch of rain on 1,000 square feet of roof is about 600 gallons. That is a lot of water.

    Restaurant and Industrial Drain Service in Boston

    Restaurants and industrial buildings send heavier loads down their drains than homes do. Interceptors need regular pumping. We jet kitchen lines between interceptor pumpings, clear floor sinks and trench drains, and keep a service log your health inspector can review if they ask about grease management.

    Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Boston, MA

    Sewer laterals fail from roots, settling, or plain age. Older clay and cast iron sewers crack at the joints, which lets roots in. We camera the line, locate the damage from the surface, and price a spot repair, a full replacement, or trenchless lining where the pipe is a good candidate. You pick.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Boston

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Boston, MA

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Boston

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Listening Through Concrete in Boston

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, and that skill only comes with lots of practice. A pressurized line gives the clearest sound in your Boston home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Boston, MA

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Boston, MA

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Boston home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Boston

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Boston

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. Guessing wrong costs far more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while a precise mark keeps the job to one opening or a reroute that leaves the slab alone in your Boston property.

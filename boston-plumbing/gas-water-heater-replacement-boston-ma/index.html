    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Boston

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Boston, MA

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Boston

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Massachusetts homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Boston

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Boston, MA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

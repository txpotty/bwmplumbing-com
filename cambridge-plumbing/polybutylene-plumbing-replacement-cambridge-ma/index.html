    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Cambridge, MA

    Some homeowners in Cambridge, MA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine.     Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Cambridge

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual.     Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow.

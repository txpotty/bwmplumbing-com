    Line Jetting Services in Cambridge, MA

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Cambridge, MA

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Main Line Jetting in Cambridge, MA

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Cambridge

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Cambridge, MA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. The difference shows on camera.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Cambridge, MA

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Cambridge, MA

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. Commercial stacks and mains get jetted outside business hours, with a written report for the manager.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Cambridge, MA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Cambridge

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. A backup closes rooms or stores. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

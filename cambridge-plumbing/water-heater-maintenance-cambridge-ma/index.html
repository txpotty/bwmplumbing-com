    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in Cambridge, MA

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Cambridge

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Cambridge

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Cambridge

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in Cambridge

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. Each brand has its own anode type, drain valve style and descaling procedure, and we follow the manufacturer's guidelines so the service holds up if you ever need to file a warranty claim. Model details matter. We bring the right parts.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Cambridge

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. We test the gas connection too.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Cambridge, MA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. CO is the risk. We also test the thermocouple.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Cambridge

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. Pans get inspected. We check venting and access.

    Tankless Descaling in Cambridge, MA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

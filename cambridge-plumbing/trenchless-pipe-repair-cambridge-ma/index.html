    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter.     Pipe Bursting Services in Cambridge, MA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Cambridge, MA soil.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Cambridge, MA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. BWM Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

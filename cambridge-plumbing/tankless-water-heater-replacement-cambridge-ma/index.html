    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Cambridge, MA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Cambridge

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Cambridge, MA

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Cambridge

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. That number picks the unit. We handle the gas, water and vent changes needed to bring the old installation up to current code.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Cambridge

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Cambridge

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Cambridge, MA

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Cambridge

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Cambridge, MA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Cambridge, MA

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Cambridge

    Residential ejector systems in Cambridge serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Cambridge

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in Massachusetts, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and more expensive. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Cambridge, MA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Cambridge

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Their packaged systems come with the basin, lid, pump and check valve matched together, which cuts down on field fitting and gives the inspector a clean, listed assembly to look at. A full-port valve gets added.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Cambridge

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. They need a vent too.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Cambridge

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Cambridge, MA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Cambridge

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Cambridge

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Cambridge, MA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Close the stops or main while you wait. Leave the tub drain open.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Cambridge

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

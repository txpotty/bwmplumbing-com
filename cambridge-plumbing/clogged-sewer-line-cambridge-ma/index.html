    What Our Sewer Clearing Covers in Cambridge, MA

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Cambridge

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Cambridge

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale while the nozzle drives itself forward. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Cambridge, MA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Cambridge, MA

    Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in Massachusetts, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and more expensive. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. Then we quote a spot repair.

    Commercial Clogged Sewer Line Service in Cambridge

    Commercial sewer lines in Cambridge carry far more than a house does. Kitchen grease is the big one. BWM Plumbing clears restaurant mains, apartment building laterals and shared office drains with commercial-grade sectional machines and a high-flow jetter, and we can set up a jetting schedule to keep the line open between grease trap pumpings.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Cambridge, MA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. We walk you through it.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Cambridge

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. That beats a 2 a.m. backup. BWM Plumbing sets up scheduled cutting or jetting for homes in Cambridge with older clay or cast iron, typically on a one- to two-year cycle based on regrowth. Each visit includes a quick camera check.

    Older Cambridge homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. The joints are the weak point. BWM Plumbing cuts roots out with a sectional machine and a root saw sized to the pipe, then jets the line to wash out the cut roots. Root treatment can slow regrowth between visits.

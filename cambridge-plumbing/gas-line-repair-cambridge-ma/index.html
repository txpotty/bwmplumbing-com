    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Cambridge, MA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Cambridge, MA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Cambridge

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Cambridge building officials.     Propane Gas Line Service in Cambridge, MA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Cambridge, MA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Cambridge

Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Cambridge

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Cambridge, MA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops.     Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Cambridge

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Cambridge, MA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BWM Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

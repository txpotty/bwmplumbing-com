    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look.     Emergency Tankless Repair in Cambridge, MA

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Most no-heat calls come down to a failed igniter or an exchanger so scaled that the high-limit switch keeps tripping. We usually fix it on the first trip, since igniters and descaling gear ride along.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Cambridge

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Cambridge

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Cambridge, MA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Cambridge

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Cambridge, MA

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Heavy scale or a blocked exhaust is the usual cause. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Cambridge

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

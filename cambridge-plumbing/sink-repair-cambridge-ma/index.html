    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Cambridge, MA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Cambridge

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Cambridge, MA

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Cambridge

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Cambridge, MA

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Cambridge

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Cambridge, MA

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

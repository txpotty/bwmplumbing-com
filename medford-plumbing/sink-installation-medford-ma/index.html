    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Medford, MA

    Vessel Sink Installation in Medford, MA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Medford

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. We install stainless sinks with commercial faucets and basket strainers, mount them to carry a full bowl of water plus a stack of dishes, and add a hand sink where your inspector wants one. We schedule around when you are open. Night installs are possible.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Medford

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. A cast iron farmhouse bowl can top a hundred pounds empty, so the base cabinet often gets reinforced before it goes in. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Medford, MA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Medford

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. We install wall-hung and countertop units on carriers, add sensor or metering faucets, and set thermostatic mixing valves where code requires tempered water at public hand sinks. Vandal-resistant parts are available. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Medford

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Medford, MA

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Medford, MA

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

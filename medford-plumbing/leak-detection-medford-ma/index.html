    Walls, Slabs and Yards: Leak Detection in Medford, MA

    Slab Leak Detection in Medford

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. Look for warm tile first. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Medford, MA

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Medford

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. A thermal scan shows the wet patch, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.

    Gas Leak Detection in Medford

    A faint rotten-egg odor by the furnace or stove needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Medford, MA

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, because those parts often fail together. It is a short visit.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Medford

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.

    Pool Leak Detection in Medford

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Medford, MA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

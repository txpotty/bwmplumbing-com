    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Medford

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Medford, MA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Medford

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Medford, MA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks.     Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Medford

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Medford

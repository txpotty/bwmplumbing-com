    Why Choose BWM Plumbing for Toilet Installation in Medford, MA?

    Most leaking toilets we get called back to were installed by someone else with the flange too low or the bowl never shimmed. Stacking two wax rings is the usual shortcut. It fails. We use a flange extender or a thicker foam seal instead, sized to the real gap.

    • Right Tools, A level, composite shims and a light hand on the bolts keep the porcelain from cracking.
    • Full Testing, Five or more flushes and a dry paper towel at every joint before we pack up.
    • Durability, Stainless bolts and braided supply lines hold up far longer than plated steel and vinyl.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Medford

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Medford

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Medford, MA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Medford

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Medford, MA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Medford

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Medford, MA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Medford

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

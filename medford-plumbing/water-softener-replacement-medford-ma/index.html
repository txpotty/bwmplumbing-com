    Water Softener Work in Medford, MA

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Medford

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Medford, MA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Medford, MA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Medford

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Medford, MA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Medford, MA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Massachusetts homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Medford, MA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Massachusetts homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Medford

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

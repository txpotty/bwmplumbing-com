    Annual Tankless Checkup in Medford, MA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Medford, MA

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Medford

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Medford, MA

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Medford, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Medford, MA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Medford

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Medford, MA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Medford

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Medford

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.     Grease Line Jetting in Medford

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Medford, MA

    Sometimes the trap is fine and the problem is downstream. A camera tells us quickly. A trap that is half empty while floor sinks keep backing up points to a clog in the lateral toward the main, which needs jetting, not another pump-out. We can jet on the same visit.

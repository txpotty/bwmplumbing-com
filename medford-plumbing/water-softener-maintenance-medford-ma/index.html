    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Medford

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. Cheaper than a new head.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Medford

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. Each visit includes a hardness test, logged for your warranty file. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Medford, MA

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.

    Resin Rebedding in Medford

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Medford

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Medford

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Medford, MA

    Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Over enough years, it can lock a cartridge in place, pit a valve body and etch glass doors past the point where cleaning helps, and that is when replacement makes sense. A softener helps the new one. We can recommend one.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Leominster

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and basements collect sand, dirt and debris. Their traps also dry out. We clear the drain and the line behind it, refill the trap and install a trap seal if it rarely gets water. A sewer odor usually means the trap is empty.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Leominster, MA

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. Hair wraps around the rod. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line, and we can replace a worn pop-up assembly or corroded tailpiece during the same visit. Mineral crust can narrow the tailpiece too.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Leominster

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on. We pull the trap, send a cable through the wall line all the way to the stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

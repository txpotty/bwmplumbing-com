    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Leominster, MA

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Leominster

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Leominster

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Leominster, MA

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Leominster

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Leominster, MA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Leominster, MA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Leominster

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Leominster

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Leominster

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain.     Garbage Disposal Repair in Leominster

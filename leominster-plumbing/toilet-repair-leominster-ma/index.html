    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Leominster, MA

    Running Toilet Repair in Leominster

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Leominster

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We replace them with an adjustable modern fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Leominster

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far. Epoxy does not hold under water pressure for long. We replace it. If the tank model is still made, a matching tank is often cheaper than a new toilet.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Leominster

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper.     Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Leominster

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall.     Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Leominster, MA

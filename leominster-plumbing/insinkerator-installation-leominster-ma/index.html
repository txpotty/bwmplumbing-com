    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Leominster, MA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

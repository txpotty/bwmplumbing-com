    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Leominster, MA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Leominster, MA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Leominster

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Shutting off and draining outdoor faucets in the fall, and keeping cabinet doors open during cold snaps, prevents a lot of winter damage. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. A vacuum breaker goes on every new one.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Leominster

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Leominster

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Leominster

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Leominster, MA

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Leominster, MA

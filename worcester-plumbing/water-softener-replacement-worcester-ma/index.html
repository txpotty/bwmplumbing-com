    Swapping a salt-based softener in Massachusetts homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Worcester

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

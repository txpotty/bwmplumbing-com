    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Worcester, MA

    Control Valve Repair in Worcester

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. BWM Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Worcester, MA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Worcester

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. BWM Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Worcester, MA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Worcester

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Worcester

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority. The crew in Worcester puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Usually a stuck piston or a dead motor is to blame, and both can be replaced the same day.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Worcester, MA

    Drips around fittings waste water. We trace it to the seal, O-ring, hose or internal part that failed. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Worcester, MA home and test it under line pressure.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Worcester, MA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

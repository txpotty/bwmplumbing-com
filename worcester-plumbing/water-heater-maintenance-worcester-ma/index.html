    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Worcester

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. The tank gets drained until it runs clear, then refilled, and the anode, relief valve and connections get checked. Takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Anode Rod Checks in Worcester

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Worcester, MA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Worcester

    Businesses cannot run without hot water.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Worcester, MA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. Carbon monoxide is the concern. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.

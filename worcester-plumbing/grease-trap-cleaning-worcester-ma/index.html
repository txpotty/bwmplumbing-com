    Why Choose BWM Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Worcester, MA?

    A full trap lets grease out the outlet tee. From there it hardens in the line. We pump on a schedule that keeps the grease and solids layer well under the 25 percent mark, measured with a sludge judge at every visit rather than guessed from the calendar.

    • Prevent Blockages, Regular pumping keeps floor drains flowing during the dinner rush.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.
    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the sour, rotten-egg smell that decaying grease gives off.

    Citywide Coverage

    Our routes cover Worcester and the towns around it on a regular rotation. Kitchens outside town ride the same route. Being on a set route keeps scheduled visits on the date we promised, which matters when an inspector expects a pump-out every 30 or 60 days.

    Experienced Team

    The same crew tends to run the same route. They learn your kitchen. After a few visits they know where the cleanout key is kept, which sink drains the fryer wash water, and what time your dish crew starts so they can stay out of the way.

    Safety-First Approach

    An open interceptor hatch in a parking lot is a serious fall hazard. We set cones before lifting it. The lid stays off only as long as the hose is in the tank, and before we leave we check that it is seated flat and, on locking lids, bolted down.

    What We Clean in Worcester, MA

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Worcester

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Room to work is tight. Mats go down first, the contents get pumped or scooped into sealed containers, and the floor gets wiped so prep can start right after. Gasket and flow control get checked too.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Worcester, MA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. With the tank empty, we inspect the tees, baffle wall, risers and lid seals, since a cracked riser lets dirt and groundwater into the tank. Lids go back on tight.

    Commercial Grease Trap Pumping in Worcester

    Fryer-heavy menus load a trap fastest. Two weeks is enough for a wings place to fill a 50-gallon trap. Our trucks carry enough hose to reach traps tucked behind walk-ins or down in a basement mechanical room, and enough tank capacity for interceptors well over 1,000 gallons. We pump every one to the floor.

    After-Hours Grease Trap Calls in Worcester

    Backups do not wait for business hours. Nights and weekends, we still answer trap emergencies. The first job is stopping the water, which usually means pumping the trap down, and the second is figuring out whether the blockage is in the trap, the outlet tee or the line downstream. Most kitchens are back open by the next service.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Worcester, MA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Worcester, MA

    Cost follows trap size and fill level. A 20-gallon under-sink trap is a short visit. A 1,500-gallon interceptor that has gone five months without pumping needs a vacuum truck, a pressure wand and extra disposal fees for the volume, so it lands at the high end. A steady schedule lowers what each visit costs.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Worcester, MA

    Sometimes the trap is fine and the problem is downstream. We find that out fast with a camera. A trap that is half empty while floor sinks keep backing up points to a clog in the lateral toward the main, which needs jetting, not another pump-out. We can jet on the same visit.

    Keeping Grease Trap Costs Down in Worcester, MA

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. We carry common sizes. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Worcester

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Worcester, MA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks.     Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Worcester

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Worcester

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

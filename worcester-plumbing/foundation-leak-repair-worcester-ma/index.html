    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in Worcester, MA

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Worcester, MA

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. We dig a pit beside the foundation and hand-tunnel to the leak, which works well when the break sits under cabinets, a tiled shower or hardwood you do not want torn up. BWM Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Worcester, MA repair is done.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Worcester

    Not every wet foundation is a plumbing leak. Gutters cause plenty. Downspouts that dump right at the wall and soil that slopes toward the house push water against the foundation, and that pressure finds every crack in the concrete. We regrade the soil, lengthen the downspouts and install French drains around Worcester homes when needed.

    Water Line Reroute in Worcester

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Worcester, MA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we explain each one.

    Same-Day Foundation Leak Response in Worcester, MA

    A large leak under the slab can flood floors and soak the ground fast. Close the main valve. Over the phone we help you find the shutoff, then a crew locates the break and isolates that one line so the rest of the house keeps water.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Worcester, MA

    Older homes in Worcester, MA often have soft copper running under the slab. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can slowly eat through copper pipe. Copper usually pinholes where it rubs on rock. The bad section comes out, new Type L copper goes in with sweated or pressed joints, and the full line gets a pressure test before any dirt goes back.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Worcester, MA

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. That's the main tool. BWM Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, so in most homes we can mark the leak within a foot or two, in Worcester, MA or anywhere else.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Worcester

    Locating comes before anything else. First we isolate hot from cold. Each side gets its own pressure test, and once we know which side is losing water, we follow the pipe route with a tracer and listen along it with the ground mic until the sound peaks. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Worcester

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. We read the tag first, since it lists what the utility found. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day or two.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Worcester, MA

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.

    Gas Line Repair and Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. The joint gets broken, cleaned and remade with new pipe dope rated for gas. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a gauge test.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Worcester

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. Regulator vents and lockup pressure get checked along with every joint. Rooftop units leak too. Where downtime costs you money, we isolate only the affected branch and keep the rest of the building running. Staff get a short walkthrough on the shutoff locations.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Worcester

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. When the leak is small enough, we plan the shutdown for after close. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. We test every hose and manifold joint and leave tags on anything we had to shut off. New pipe still needs the commercial inspection.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Worcester, MA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Gas Leak Detection in Worcester

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. Slow leaks are what the test gauge is for. We isolate, then sniff. Testing happens with the appliance valves shut and again with them open, since some leaks hide past a valve. Finding the exact joint keeps the job small.

    How We Install Showers in Worcester, MA

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Worcester, MA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. No high curbs. ADA layouts are available on request.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Worcester, MA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. Good for rentals and guest baths. Usually a one-day job.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Worcester

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Worcester, MA

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. We install commercial valves with tamper-resistant trim, vandal-resistant heads and drains sized for several showers running at once, all to commercial code. Scheduling fits your hours. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Shower Stall Installation in Worcester

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Worcester, MA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Worcester

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Worcester, MA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

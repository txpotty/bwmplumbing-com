    Main Line and Sewer Cleaning in Worcester, MA

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Worcester

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Root Masses in Worcester Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Worcester

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Tree Root Removal in Worcester, MA Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Worcester, MA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Worcester

    The main carries everything out of the house. When it stops, nothing drains. We run heavy sectional cable with a cutter sized for 3 or 4 inch pipe, working from the cleanout toward the street until the head turns freely and water runs at full speed, which is common work in the older homes around Worcester.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Worcester, MA

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Worcester, MA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

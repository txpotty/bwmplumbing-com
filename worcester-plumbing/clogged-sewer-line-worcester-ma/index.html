    Sewer Camera Inspection in Worcester

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Worcester

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. That beats a 2 a.m. backup. BWM Plumbing sets up scheduled cutting or jetting for homes in Worcester with older clay or cast iron, usually every 12 to 24 months depending on how fast the roots come back. Each visit includes a quick camera check.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Worcester

    Older Worcester homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. The joints are the weak point. BWM Plumbing cuts roots out with a sectional machine and a root saw sized to the pipe, then follows with a jetter to flush the cut pieces and clear the fine roots the saw misses. Root treatment can slow regrowth between visits.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Worcester

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Many times they team up. Grease coats the pipe, wipes snag on the coating and roots growing through a joint hold the whole mass in place until the line stops. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Worcester, MA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. More than one usually means the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Commercial Sewer Line Clearing in Worcester, MA

    A backed up sewer at a restaurant or office means closing the doors. That gets expensive fast. BWM Plumbing clears commercial mains with heavy sectional machines and a high-flow jetter, scheduling the work for off hours when that helps. Grease-heavy lines get jetted, not just cabled.

    Emergency Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Worcester, MA

    A fully blocked sewer will push raw sewage out the lowest drain in the building. Usually a floor drain or tub. BWM Plumbing sends an emergency tech across Worcester, MA with the full kit, clears the line from the cleanout and contains the spill so it does not spread across more flooring. The spill area gets cleaned.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Worcester, MA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Worcester

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Worcester yard.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Worcester

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Worcester, MA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Worcester, MA soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Worcester

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

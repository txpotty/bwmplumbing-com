    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Worcester, MA

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Worcester

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Bad sections get cut out and replaced, and a run with repeated pinholes gets repiped rather than patched. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Relief in Worcester

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. A sub-slab drain line to a sump drops the water table right under the floor. It is not a quick job.

    New Sump Pump Installation in Worcester

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Exterior Membrane Waterproofing in Worcester, MA

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Water heaters and boilers run long hours through the winter, which shortens their life compared to milder parts of the country. The membrane is matched to those conditions, the footing drain is replaced, and the backfill is graded to slope away. Downspouts get extended too.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Worcester, MA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Plan on several days. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Worcester, MA

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. A tank leaking at the seam is done. Valve and connector leaks are usually a same-visit fix, and an automatic shutoff sensor is worth adding.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Worcester, MA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Worcester

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are common and often stay dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Rocky glacial soil and shallow ledge are common in Massachusetts, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and more expensive. We inject leaking cracks from the inside with polyurethane foam, which expands into the crack and stays flexible as the wall moves. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Where We Find Leaks in Worcester, MA

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Worcester

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Worcester, MA

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either one can waste thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. We start outside. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Worcester, MA

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. Our tech runs a camera from the cleanout to the city tap, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Worcester

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. A thermal scan shows the wet patch, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Worcester, MA

    Water coming through a ceiling at night does not wait for an appointment. Shut the main. We respond to Worcester, MA emergencies with full detection gear, isolate the failed line and restore water to the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Gas Leak Detection in Worcester

    A faint rotten-egg odor by the furnace or stove needs a professional check. Get out first if it is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Pool Leak Detection in Worcester

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Worcester, MA

Each zone gets isolated and tested, then mark leaks for repair. Tenants usually never notice.

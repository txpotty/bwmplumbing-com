    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Worcester

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Massachusetts homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Worcester

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Worcester, MA

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. Downtime stays short.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Worcester, MA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Worcester, MA

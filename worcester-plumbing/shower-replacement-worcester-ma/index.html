    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Worcester

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. The tub comes out, the drain is adjusted and the valve goes in at the right height with grab bar blocking and a handheld. No tall curb. It is one of the safest changes you can make.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. Demo goes to the studs, damaged wood gets replaced and a new waterproofed pan goes in with the valve set to tile depth. The slope has to be right. We flood-test before tile.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Worcester

    Old valves waste water and swing temperature. We replace corroded valves with modern pressure-balancing or thermostatic models, usually working through an access panel on the back side of the wall so the tile stays in place. A bigger trim plate hides the old hole. It works alone or as part of a rebuild.

    Shower Pan Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. On slab homes, water can travel under the flooring and show up as staining or odors in a hallway, so we trace it before we start tearing anything out. Then we remove the pan. The new pan gets proper slope, waterproofing and a flood test.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Worcester

    Old bathtubs that nobody uses take up the best spot in the bathroom. The tub comes out, the drain is rerouted and the valve goes up to shower height before walls and glass go in. Stepping in gets easier. That is a big deal for many households.

    Rental and Hotel Shower Replacement in Worcester

    Rental showers need to be durable and easy to fix. We use commercial parts and the same cartridge in every unit, which keeps repairs fast. Work fits tenant schedules. Turnovers are a good time to do it.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Worcester

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. Cracks tend to start where the base was not supported, and each shower flexes it until water slips through to the subfloor. We pull the old unit. The subfloor gets checked and the new base goes on mortar.

    Budget Shower Replacement in Worcester, MA

    Replacing just the shower can freshen a bathroom without a full remodel. An acrylic unit and a new valve cost far less than tile and hold up for years when set right. You see each option's price. The choice is yours.

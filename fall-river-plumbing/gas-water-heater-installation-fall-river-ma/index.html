    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Fall River, MA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Fall River

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Fall River, MA

    Price starts lowest with an atmospheric vent tank. Power vent and tankless run higher, and older homes may need vent or gas line work. Permits and haul-away are included. We also compare gas and electric operating costs.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Fall River

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Fall River

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We call the propane company when needed. Every joint is leak tested.

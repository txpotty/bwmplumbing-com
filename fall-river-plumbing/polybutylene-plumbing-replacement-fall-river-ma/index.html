    Why Polybutylene Fails in Fall River

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is corrosive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

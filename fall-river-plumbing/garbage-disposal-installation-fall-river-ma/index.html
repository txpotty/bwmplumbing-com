    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Fall River

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Fall River, MA

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Fall River, MA

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Fall River

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Fall River

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Fall River homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

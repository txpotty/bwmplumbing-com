    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Fall River, MA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast.     ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Fall River

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Fall River

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Fall River

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Fall River, MA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Fall River, MA

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. BWM Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Fall River

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Fall River, MA

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Fall River, MA

    Besides repairs, BWM Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load. Then we coordinate with the water provider on the tap and meter change, set the backflow assembly and get the inspection signed off, so the tenant is not held up waiting on paperwork.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Fall River

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Fall River, MA

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. No water means no open sign. BWM Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, with large-diameter couplings and fittings already loaded. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Fall River

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Fall River

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

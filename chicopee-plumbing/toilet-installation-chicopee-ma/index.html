    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Chicopee, MA

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots.     Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Chicopee, MA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Chicopee, MA

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Chicopee, MA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Chicopee

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. Without an outlet tee, the grease cap flows right into the sewer, and older concrete interceptors lose that tee more often than you would think. We can often fix small problems on the spot.

    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Chicopee, MA

    Gray water coming up through the floor sinks in the middle of service is the classic sign the trap has hit capacity. Same-day trucks are the goal on emergency calls. First the trap gets pumped, and then we check the line past the outlet, since a lateral packed with grease will not clear just from pumping. Sometimes it needs jetting too.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Chicopee

    Indoor traps usually sit under the three-compartment sink or in a recessed pit near the dish station. Space is tight back there. We lay down mats, pump or hand-scoop the contents into sealed containers, and wipe down the floor before we leave so the kitchen can go straight back to prep. We also check the gasket and the flow control.

    Grease Line Jetting in Chicopee

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Chicopee

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. A hard grease cap gets broken up with a pressure wand before pumping, otherwise it plugs the suction hose and half of it stays in the tank. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Chicopee, MA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. With the tank empty, we inspect the tees, baffle wall, risers and lid seals, since a cracked riser lets dirt and groundwater into the tank. Lids go back on tight.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Chicopee, MA

    Homes rarely have a grease trap, though a few large custom kitchens and some home-based catering setups in Chicopee, MA put a small unit under the sink. These are usually 10 to 20 gallon units. We pull the lid, scoop out the grease and solids by hand, clean the baffle and reset the gasket so it does not leak or smell. About thirty minutes.

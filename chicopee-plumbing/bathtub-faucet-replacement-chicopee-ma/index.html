    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Chicopee, MA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Chicopee

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Chicopee

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Chicopee

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

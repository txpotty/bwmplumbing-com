    French Drain Installation in Chicopee

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. Filter fabric around the stone keeps silt out. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Dry Well Installation in Chicopee, MA

    Where there is no place to discharge on the surface, a dry well lets water soak into deeper soil. Drainage depends on the soil. We run a percolation check before digging, size the well for the roof or yard area feeding it, and keep it at least ten feet from the foundation and well away from any septic field.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Chicopee

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Restaurants need it most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. Our test covers the float, the check valve and the discharge line. Discharge must go outside. Many sump floods come from a blocked discharge line, not a dead pump.

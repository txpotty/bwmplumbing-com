    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Chicopee

    CIPP lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot. Bursting runs a bit less per foot. Pipe bursting often runs $60 to $200 per foot plus the access pits, and a typical residential sewer line totals $4,000 to $15,000 depending on length and condition.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting swaps the pipe out. With CIPP, a resin-soaked liner cures against the old pipe wall, while bursting breaks the old pipe apart and pulls new HDPE into its place, leaving a completely new pipe.

    An epoxy liner, correctly installed, is rated to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    Compared to digging, yes. Far less dirt is disturbed. Trenchless repair leaves trees, plants and soil in place, and because the old pipe usually stays in the ground as the host, very little material ends up hauled to a landfill.

    Cast iron, clay, PVC and concrete can all be lined. The pipe's shape counts. If the pipe has collapsed or shifted badly, bursting may suit it better than lining, and the camera tells us which.

    Many cities want a permit for sewer replacement, trenchless included. We check the local rules and file for permits when needed.

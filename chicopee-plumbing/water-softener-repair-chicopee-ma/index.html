    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Chicopee, MA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Chicopee, MA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Chicopee, MA

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. We test it under pressure.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Chicopee, MA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

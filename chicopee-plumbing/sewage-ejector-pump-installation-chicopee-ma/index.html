    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Chicopee

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. They need a vent too.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Chicopee, MA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Chicopee

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Nor'easters and heavy spring rain can raise groundwater quickly in Massachusetts, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. Backup power can help.

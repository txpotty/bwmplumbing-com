    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Peabody, MA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Over years the opening shrinks. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Peabody, MA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Peabody

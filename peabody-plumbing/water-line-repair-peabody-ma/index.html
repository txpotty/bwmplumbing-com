    What Our Water Line Repair Covers in Peabody, MA

    Main Water Line Replacement in Peabody, MA

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Peabody

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Peabody

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Peabody

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. We dig where the noise is loudest.

    Underground Leak Detection in Peabody, MA

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Tree roots and shifting fill around old clay or cast iron sewer laterals are a frequent cause of backups in established neighborhoods. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Peabody, MA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Same-day repairs are the norm. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Peabody

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Renovations frequently uncover generations of patchwork plumbing, so repiping often makes more sense than repairing one section at a time. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Peabody, MA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Peabody, MA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Peabody

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Peabody

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Peabody, MA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time.     Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Peabody, MA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Peabody

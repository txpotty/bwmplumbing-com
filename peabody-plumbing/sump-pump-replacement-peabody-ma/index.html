    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Peabody, MA Home

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Peabody, MA

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Peabody, MA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Peabody

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Peabody, MA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Peabody, MA

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (857) 335-4894 and we will send someone. Leave the lid off, get cords off the wet floor, and run a wet vac to keep the water level below the top of the pit while you wait. Common pumps ride on the truck, so most swaps happen right then. We also check why the old one failed.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Peabody

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Peabody, MA

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    Backup Pump Options in Peabody, MA

    Snowmelt combined with rain is a common recipe for wet basements, overworked sump pumps and municipal sewer backups. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. The backup kicks on if the primary dies or the power goes out. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. They will not work on a well.

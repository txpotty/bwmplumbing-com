    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in New Bedford

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in New Bedford, MA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in New Bedford, MA

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Tankless Descaling in New Bedford, MA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Much of the public water in Massachusetts is soft and slightly acidic, which is easy on fixtures but can gradually wear through copper pipe. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. We also check the burner and venting.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in New Bedford

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. Efficiency comes back. So does recovery time.

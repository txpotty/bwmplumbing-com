    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in New Bedford

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in New Bedford

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass.

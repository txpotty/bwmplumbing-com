    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in New Bedford

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate.     Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in New Bedford

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in New Bedford

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

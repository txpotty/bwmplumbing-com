    Residential Foundation Leak Repair in New Bedford, MA

    For a homeowner in New Bedford, MA, the first question is usually how bad it is. We answer that with a pressure test.     Foundation Leak Detection in New Bedford

    Locating comes before anything else. First we isolate hot from cold. Hot and cold get tested separately, then we trace the losing line and walk it with the ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there.     Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in New Bedford, MA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we explain each one.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in New Bedford

    Slab leaks show up quietly. Sometimes all you get is a warm spot on the tile or a bill that creeps up. BWM Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to locate the break under your concrete. For New Bedford homes, the fix might be a spot repair through the floor, a reroute through the attic, or a tunnel from outside, depending on where the leak sits and how old the pipe is.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in New Bedford

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. A small opening gets cut, the pipe is exposed and repaired, the line is tested, and the hole is backfilled in layers and patched level. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

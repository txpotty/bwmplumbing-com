    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in New Bedford, MA

    Commercial Leak Detection in New Bedford, MA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in New Bedford

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. We repair leaks, replace water heaters, clear stacks that serve several floors and repipe whole buildings while keeping residents informed about every shutoff. Pricing stays consistent by unit. Invoices list each unit number, so your accounting team can bill back to the right account.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in New Bedford

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in New Bedford, MA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in New Bedford

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in New Bedford

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in New Bedford, MA

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Scale cuts output fast. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

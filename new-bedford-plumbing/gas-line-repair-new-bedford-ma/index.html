    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in New Bedford, MA

    CSST Repair in New Bedford

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in New Bedford

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in New Bedford, MA.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in New Bedford, MA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Propane Gas Line Service in New Bedford, MA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Jet Pump Replacement in New Bedford, MA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in New Bedford

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. Soft starts also cut wear on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in New Bedford, MA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in New Bedford, MA

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. Labor and hoist time, along with new wire and pipe, make up the rest. You see every line item.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in New Bedford, MA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in New Bedford

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in New Bedford

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. The suction line gets a pressure test. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

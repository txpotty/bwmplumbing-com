    Standard Toilet Replacement in New Bedford

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in New Bedford

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in New Bedford, MA

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush.     Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in New Bedford

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in New Bedford, MA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in New Bedford

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in New Bedford, MA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. You get our honest take on which models to skip. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

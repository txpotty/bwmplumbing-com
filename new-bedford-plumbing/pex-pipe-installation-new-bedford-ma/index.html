    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in New Bedford

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in New Bedford

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in New Bedford, MA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

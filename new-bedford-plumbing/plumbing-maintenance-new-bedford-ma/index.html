    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in New Bedford, MA

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.

    Hot Water Tank Service in New Bedford, MA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in New Bedford

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Water Softener Service in New Bedford

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Hidden Leak Checks in New Bedford, MA

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines and old lead service connections are still found in multi-family houses and older single-family homes. Early finds prevent mold. Usually the fix is minor.

    Repipe Assessments in New Bedford, MA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. PEX and copper are the options. Repiping can be done in stages.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in New Bedford, MA

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Frozen pipes are a real winter risk across Massachusetts, especially during stretches of single-digit nights and strong wind. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Pipe sleeves cost little. Burst pipes are not.

    Sump Pump Testing in New Bedford, MA

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Nor'easters and heavy spring rain can raise groundwater quickly in Massachusetts, sending water into basements that stay dry most of the year. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in New Bedford, MA

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

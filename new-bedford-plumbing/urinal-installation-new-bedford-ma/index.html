    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in New Bedford, MA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in New Bedford

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in New Bedford

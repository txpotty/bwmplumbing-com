    Grease Trap Leak Repair in New Bedford

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. A hairline crack can sometimes be welded.     Flow Control Device Repair in New Bedford

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate.     Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in New Bedford, MA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in New Bedford, MA

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling.     Mid-shift backups usually come from a plugged inlet or a trap that is overdue for pumping. We can often pump it and clear the inlet in under two hours. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

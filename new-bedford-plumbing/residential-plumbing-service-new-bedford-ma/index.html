    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in New Bedford, MA

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most.     Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in New Bedford

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. We patch the access holes, ready for paint.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Backups in New Bedford

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Roots are common in older clay lines.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in New Bedford

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. You get the video.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in New Bedford, MA

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in New Bedford, MA

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage.     Water Softener and Filter Service in New Bedford

    Pinhole leaks in older copper lines are a familiar problem where the water is aggressive, and they tend to show up in clusters. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater.
